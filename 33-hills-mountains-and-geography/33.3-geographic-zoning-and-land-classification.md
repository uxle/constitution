# Section 33.3 — Geographic Zoning and Land Classification

## Articles

**Article 33.3.1 (National Land Classification System).** All territory is classified into zones (urban, agricultural, forested, highland, coastal, protected) with zone-specific rules that govern together with, not in place of, local government authority under Part 07.

**Article 33.3.2 (Rezoning Procedure).** Rezoning requires public notice, an environmental and social impact assessment, and a vote of the affected local council, preventing quiet reclassification for private benefit.

**Article 33.3.3 (Cross-Reference to Clean Cities).** Urban zones are additionally governed by the cleanliness and sanitation standards of Part 35.

*Design Note: A single classification system, applied consistently from hilltop to coastline, is what lets the rest of this Constitution's terrain-specific rules (13, 24, 32, 33, 35) actually interlock instead of contradicting each other.*

**Cross-references:** 07-local-government, 13-environment, 35-clean-cities-and-sanitation


## Implementation Detail

**Article 33.3.4 (Rezoning Timeline).** A rezoning application requires a minimum 60-day public notice period and a local council vote no sooner than 90 days after filing.
