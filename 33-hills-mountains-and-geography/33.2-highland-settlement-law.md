# Section 33.2 — Highland Settlement Law

## Articles

**Article 33.2.1 (Right to Remain).** Existing highland communities have a right to remain on ancestral land and are not to be displaced for commercial development without free, prior, and informed consent and fair compensation.

**Article 33.2.2 (Infrastructure Standards).** Highland settlements are entitled to the same baseline infrastructure standards (Part 23) as lowland settlements — roads, water, electricity, healthcare, and schools — adapted to terrain, so that "hard to reach" is never used as an excuse for permanent neglect.

**Article 33.2.3 (Disaster Preparedness).** Highland regions maintain landslide and flash-flood early-warning systems as part of Part 26 (Emergency and Crisis) planning.

*Design Note: The instinct in many existing states is to under-invest in mountainous or hilly regions because they are costly to service; this Article makes that under-investment a constitutional breach, not merely an unfortunate budget outcome.*

## Implementation Detail

**Article 33.2.4 (Infrastructure Parity Deadline).** Highland settlements must reach baseline infrastructure parity with lowland settlements of comparable population within 10 years of this Constitution's adoption, tracked in the annual Auditor-General report.
