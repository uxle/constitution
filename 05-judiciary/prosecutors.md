# Section 5.8 — The Public Prosecution Service

> **Part 05 — The Judiciary** · Articles 5.8.1 – 5.8.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section establishes the Public Prosecution Service as an independent organ, headed by the Public Prosecutor, and orders the transparency of its instructions and guidelines.*

## Article 5.8.1 — Status and Independence

1. The Public Prosecution Service shall be independent of the Government and of every other organ of the State.
2. It shall prosecute offences and shall perform the other functions conferred on it by law.
3. In the exercise of its functions it shall be objective and impartial and shall respect the rights of the persons concerned.

## Article 5.8.2 — The Public Prosecutor

1. The Public Prosecution Service shall be headed by the Public Prosecutor.
2. The Public Prosecutor shall serve a single term of seven years and shall not be reappointed.
3. The Public Prosecutor shall be appointed from among persons of recognized integrity and professional standing, in the manner provided by law.
4. The law shall provide for a deputy and for the acting head in case of vacancy.

## Article 5.8.3 — Instructions in Individual Cases

1. The Service is hierarchical, but an instruction relating to an individual case shall be given in writing and entered in the case file.
2. A prosecutor who considers an instruction unlawful shall record the objection and may request its withdrawal.

## Article 5.8.4 — Prosecution Guidelines

1. The Public Prosecutor shall issue general prosecution guidelines to ensure consistency and transparency.
2. The guidelines shall be published and shall state the criteria for prosecution, for diversion, and for the discontinuance of charges.
3. A deviation from the guidelines shall be justified in the case file.

## Article 5.8.5 — Relationship to Police and the Anti-Corruption Commission

1. The police shall investigate offences under the direction of the Public Prosecution Service.
2. The Anti-Corruption Commission shall refer prosecutable cases to the Service, which shall decide on charges independently.
3. The Service shall report annually to the Parliament on the exercise of its functions.

## Design Note

*Prosecutorial independence is calibrated rather than absolute: the hierarchy is kept, but written, filed instructions expose any pressure to abuse, the technique of the Dutch public prosecution service. Requiring the Service, not the Anti-Corruption Commission, to decide charges keeps the investigative and accusatorial functions distinct and reviewable. The single seven-year term of the Public Prosecutor spans longer than one electoral cycle, detaching prosecution policy from the rhythm of elections.*
