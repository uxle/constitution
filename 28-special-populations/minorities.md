# Section 28.4 — Minorities

> **Part 28 — Special Populations** · Articles 28.4.1 – 28.4.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section protects cultural, religious, and linguistic minorities: identity, equality with special measures, effective participation, minority-language services, and conditions for identity to flourish.*

## Article 28.4.1 — Protection of Identity

1. Persons belonging to a cultural, religious, or linguistic minority shall not be denied the right, individually or in community with others, to enjoy their own culture, to profess and practice their own religion, and to use their own language.
2. Forced assimilation is prohibited; no organ of the State shall adopt measures aimed at destroying or suppressing a minority's identity.
3. Minorities may establish and maintain their own institutions, associations, and media as the law provides.

## Article 28.4.2 — Equality and Special Measures

1. Persons belonging to minorities enjoy full equality under Article 1.2.2 (Prohibition of Discrimination).
2. Special measures to secure effective equality are admissible under Article 1.2.5 (Affirmative Measures) and are not discrimination.
3. The allocation of public support to minority cultural activities shall follow transparent, non-discriminatory criteria.

## Article 28.4.3 — Effective Participation

1. Public authorities shall create conditions for persons belonging to minorities to participate effectively in decisions concerning them, in cultural, religious, economic, and public life.
2. Consultation procedures for measures substantially affecting a minority shall be fixed by law and conducted in good faith.
3. The law may provide for representation of minorities in advisory and public bodies.

## Article 28.4.4 — Minority-Language Services

1. In areas where a minority language is used by a significant number of inhabitants, public authorities shall provide, as fixed by law: services in that language, official documents on request, and use of the language before courts and administrative organs.
2. Education in minority languages shall be provided under Article 11.1.4 (Instruction in Minority Languages).
3. No person shall be disadvantaged for using their language with public authorities.

## Article 28.4.5 — Conditions for Identity

1. Public authorities shall promote the conditions for persons belonging to minorities to maintain and develop their culture, language, and traditions.
2. Research and data on minorities shall be collected with the safeguards of Section 28.6 and respect the right to self-identification.
3. The protection of this Section extends to religious and linguistic communities that are not territorially concentrated.

## Design Note

*The core formulation restates Article 27 of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, whose individual-and-community formula allows members of minorities to enjoy culture in community without creating corporate privileges, and adds the negative guarantee that European minority instruments made explicit: no forced assimilation. The language-services clause is a demand-driven duty, fixed by law and scaled to significant use, because rigid territorial language rules can harden boundaries that effective participation is meant to soften. Consultation in good faith borrows the standard of Section 28.5, graduated to the lighter weight due to measures that affect minorities without dispossessing them, and self-identification in data collection follows the census practice that identity is reported, not assigned.*
