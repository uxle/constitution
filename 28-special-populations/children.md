# Section 28.1 — Children

> **Part 28 — Special Populations** · Articles 28.1.1 – 28.1.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section states cross-cutting duties of all institutions toward children: best interests, identity and family care, participation, and protection in institutional and digital settings.*

## Article 28.1.1 — Priority of the Child's Best Interests

1. Every organ of the State and every institution exercising public functions shall treat the child's best interests as a primary consideration in every decision affecting a child, in legislation, budgeting, administration, and adjudication alike, under Article 1.15.2 (Best Interests of the Child).
2. The assessment of the child's best interests shall be documented in decisions of significant effect.

## Article 28.1.2 — Identity, Family, and Care

1. Public authorities shall secure birth registration, a name, and a nationality for every child born within the Republic's jurisdiction.
2. Every child has the right to family care; separation shall occur only as a last resort, under the protection services of Section 22.7 (Child Protection).
3. Where necessary, alternative care shall be consistent with the child's dignity and development, under Article 1.15.5 (Non-Derogable Essentials).

## Article 28.1.3 — Participation

1. A child capable of forming views shall be heard, directly or through a representative, in every judicial and administrative proceeding affecting the child, under Article 1.15.3 (Participation of Children).
2. Information relevant to a decision affecting a child shall be provided in a form the child understands.
3. Public authorities shall give due weight to children's views in planning services for children, including schools and care facilities.

## Article 28.1.4 — The Duty of All Institutions

1. Every public institution shall prioritize children's rights in its planning, budgeting, staffing, and service design.
2. Laws and major policies affecting children shall be assessed for their impact before adoption.
3. Officials working with children shall be trained in children's rights and screened for suitability.
4. Article 16.5.4 (Voice and Welfare of the Child) is respected in every proceeding concerning a child.

## Article 28.1.5 — Protection in Institutional and Digital Settings

1. Children in schools, care facilities, detention, and health institutions enjoy special protection against violence, exploitation, and degrading treatment, under Article 1.15.4 (Protection from Violence and Exploitation).
2. Digital environments and services offered to children shall apply safety-by-design standards consistent with Part 17 (Digital Law).
3. Children's institutions shall undergo regular independent inspection, and the Ombudsman shall be accessible to every child without charge.

## Design Note

*This Section translates the rights of Section 1.15 into duties of every institution, because a child meets the State through schools, clinics, courts, and agencies, each controlling one small part of the child's world. The documentation duty in the best-interests assessment follows the Convention on the Rights of the Child practice of making the standard auditable rather than rhetorical. Impact assessment before adoption borrows the method used for environmental and fiscal policy and applies it to the population least able to lobby, and the Ombudsman's accessibility reflects the finding that children rarely complain unless the mechanism reaches out to them.*

**Cross-references:** Article 1.15.2 (Best Interests of the Child); Article 1.15.3 (Participation of Children); Article 16.5.4 (Voice and Welfare of the Child).
