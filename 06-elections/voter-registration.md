# Section 6.2 — Voter Registration

> **Part 06 — Elections and Referendums** · Articles 6.2.1 – 6.2.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section governs the registration of voters: automatic enrollment at the age of eighteen, continuous maintenance of accurate rolls, correction of errors, and the protection of registration data.*

## Article 6.2.1 — Automatic Registration

1. Every citizen shall be registered as a voter automatically upon reaching the age of eighteen years.
2. Registration shall be based on the civil register and other public records; no application, fee, or test shall be required.
3. The Electoral Commission shall notify newly registered persons of their registration and of the means of exercising the vote.

## Article 6.2.2 — Maintenance of the Rolls

1. The Electoral Commission shall maintain voter rolls that are continuous, accurate, and current.
2. The rolls shall be updated continuously from public records and through verification exercises conducted without interrupting the right to vote.
3. The rolls shall be open to public inspection for at least the period fixed by law before each election, so that errors may be discovered and corrected in time.

## Article 6.2.3 — Inspection and Correction

1. Every person may inspect the data recorded about that person in the rolls.
2. A person may request correction of erroneous data; the Electoral Commission shall decide within the period fixed by law and shall state reasons.
3. A decision refusing correction may be appealed under Article 6.6.1 (Complaints to the Electoral Commission).
4. No eligible person may be removed from the rolls except for cause stated in a reasoned decision subject to appeal.

## Article 6.2.4 — Protection of Registration Data

1. Registration data may be processed only for electoral purposes and for other purposes expressly provided by law.
2. Registration data shall not be sold, transferred for commercial use, or disclosed to political parties in a manner incompatible with the integrity and secrecy of the vote.
3. The safeguards of the law on data protection apply, and the Information Commission may exercise its supervisory powers over the rolls.

## Design Note

*Automatic registration shifts the burden of accuracy from the individual citizen to the State, eliminating the classic losses that registration deadlines and purge exercises produce. Continuous maintenance through public records avoids periodic re-enrollment drives, which historically open the door to selective manipulation of the rolls. Pairing openness with data protection ensures that the accuracy of the register and the privacy of the registered reinforce rather than undermine each other.*
