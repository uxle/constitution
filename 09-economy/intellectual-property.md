# Section 9.9 — Intellectual Property

> **Part 09 — The Economy** · Articles 9.9.1 – 9.9.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section protects creations of the mind as limited statutory rights, balanced against public access, education, research, and the public domain.*

## Article 9.9.1 — Protection of Creations

1. The law shall protect literary, scientific, and artistic works, inventions, distinctive signs, and other creations of the mind through rights of intellectual property.
2. Such rights are guarantees of property within the meaning of Article 1.8.1 (Right to property) and may not be revoked except for cause and after a fair procedure.
3. Protection shall not extend to abstract ideas, discoveries, methods of scientific thought, or mere information as such.

## Article 9.9.2 — Limited Duration and Scope

1. Exclusive rights shall be granted for limited periods and shall extend no further than the contribution of the protected creation requires.
2. Rights shall not be renewable indefinitely, and any extension of protection shall apply only prospectively.

## Article 9.9.3 — Exceptions for Education and Research

1. The law shall permit quotation, criticism, commentary, teaching, research, private use, and archival reproduction to an extent consistent with the normal exploitation of the work.
2. Exceptions shall secure access to educational materials for institutions of learning, consistent with Article 1.10.1 (Right to education), and accessible formats for persons with disabilities.

## Article 9.9.4 — Compulsory Licenses

1. In a public-health emergency, the law shall permit the grant of compulsory licenses enabling the production or importation of patented medicines, with adequate remuneration of the rightsholder.
2. Compulsory licenses may otherwise be granted only for refusal to deal on reasonable terms, for anticompetitive conduct established under Article 9.2.1 (Protection of Competition), or for other public interests defined by law.
3. The availability of compulsory licenses shall not prejudice the affordability safeguards for medicines under Article 12.5.4 (Access and Pricing Abuse).

## Article 9.9.5 — The Public Domain

1. Material not protected, or no longer protected, by intellectual property rights belongs to the public domain and may not be enclosed against uses permitted by law by technical or contractual means.
2. The law shall provide for open access to publications and data resulting from research financed substantially by public funds.

## Design Note

*The minimum-standards frame follows the TRIPS Agreement and the Berne Convention, while the limitations clause adopts the Berne three-step test as its discipline. Compulsory licensing in public-health emergencies implements the flexibilities confirmed by the Doha Declaration on the TRIPS Agreement and Public Health, giving the constitutional right to health priority over exclusive rights in defined circumstances. Open-access duties respond to the argument that publicly financed research should be publicly readable. The accepted trade-off is between innovation incentives and affordable access, managed here through mandatory exceptions rather than permanent weakening of rights.*
