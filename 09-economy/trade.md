# Section 9.10 — Trade

> **Part 09 — The Economy** · Articles 9.10.1 – 9.10.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section guarantees freedom of external trade, reserves tariffs and trade measures to the law, and disciplines trade agreements and export controls.*

## Article 9.10.1 — Freedom of External Trade

1. Trade with foreign countries shall be free; no restriction may be imposed except by law.
2. The territory of the Republic forms a single customs territory, in which no internal customs, duties, or obstacles to the movement of goods may be established.

## Article 9.10.2 — Tariffs by Law

1. Customs duties and other charges on imports and exports may be imposed only by or under a law, and their proceeds shall form part of the public revenue governed by Section 8.1.
2. Tariffs and charges shall be applied equally to like goods from all trading partners, except preferences accorded under a customs union or free-trade agreement approved by Parliament, consistent with Article 19.5.2 (Tariffs and trade measures).
3. Measures affecting imported goods shall not discriminate between imports and domestic goods of the same class.

## Article 9.10.3 — Trade Agreements

1. Trade agreements shall be negotiated and approved in accordance with Article 19.2.2 (Parliamentary approval) before ratification and shall be published as required for all treaties.
2. Commitments negotiated with foreign partners shall be implemented only through measures adopted with domestic democratic authority.

## Article 9.10.4 — Export Controls

1. Export restrictions may be imposed by law only for security, non-proliferation, the prevention of grave humanitarian harm, or the fulfillment of obligations under international agreements, and shall be proportionate and time-limited.
2. Export restrictions shall be applied without discrimination among destinations except as required by the international obligations of the Republic.
3. The Government shall report annually to Parliament on the exercise of these powers, and restrictions shall lapse when the grounds for them cease to exist.

## Article 9.10.5 — Trade Defense

1. Anti-dumping, countervailing, and safeguard measures may be adopted only upon an investigation establishing the conditions fixed by law, for a limited period, and no broader than necessary.
2. Interested parties shall have the right to be heard in the investigation and to seek judicial review of any measure adopted.

## Design Note

*The non-discrimination clauses codify the most-favored-nation and national-treatment principles of the GATT and World Trade Organization system, and the unity of the customs territory follows the common-commercial-policy model of the European Union. Requiring parliamentary approval of trade agreements answers the democratic-deficit critique of negotiated commitments by making domestic adoption the gate through which every concession must pass. Narrow, non-discriminatory export controls keep security exceptions from becoming disguised protectionism. The accepted trade-off is a slower and more politically exposed trade policy, accepted as the price of accountability.*
