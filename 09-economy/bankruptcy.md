# Section 9.8 — Bankruptcy

> **Part 09 — The Economy** · Articles 9.8.1 – 9.8.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section guarantees honest debtors an orderly, collective path through insolvency and creditors equal treatment, preferring reorganization where a viable enterprise can be preserved.*

## Article 9.8.1 — Access to Insolvency Proceedings

1. A debtor unable to pay debts as they fall due shall have access to insolvency proceedings under a uniform law enacted by Parliament.
2. Proceedings shall be opened without undue delay upon the application of the debtor or of a creditor, and the estate of the debtor shall be administered for the collective benefit of creditors.
3. The opening of proceedings shall stay individual enforcement actions against the estate.

## Article 9.8.2 — Equal Treatment of Creditors

1. Creditors of the same rank shall be treated equally and shall share distributions in proportion to their claims; priorities shall exist only as fixed by law.
2. Claims of employees for remuneration earned before the opening of proceedings shall enjoy the priority assigned to them under Article 10.3.4 (Timely Payment).
3. Creditors shall be notified of the opening of proceedings and of the periods fixed for filing their claims.

## Article 9.8.3 — Reorganization

1. Before liquidation, the law shall offer viable debtors a reorganization procedure enabling continuation of the enterprise where it promises better satisfaction of creditors than immediate liquidation.
2. A reorganization plan may bind dissenting creditors only where they receive at least what liquidation would yield to them and the plan is confirmed by a court.

## Article 9.8.4 — Discharge of Honest Debtors

1. An honest natural-person debtor shall receive discharge of residual debts after a reasonable period fixed by law, not longer than necessary to protect creditors and deter abuse.
2. Discharge shall not extend to debts arising from fraud, willful injury, or claims excluded by law.

## Article 9.8.5 — Administration and Review

1. Insolvency proceedings shall be conducted by a qualified administrator under judicial supervision, with duties to report to the creditors and to the court.
2. The costs of administration shall be transparent and proportionate to the estate.
3. The law shall provide for recognition of, and cooperation with, foreign insolvency proceedings consistent with the international obligations of the Republic.

## Design Note

*The reorganization-first model reflects Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code; the equal-treatment and stay rules follow the German Insolvenzordnung tradition; and the cross-border clause adapts the UNCITRAL Model Law on Cross-Border Insolvency. The fresh-start discharge accepts creditor losses in exchange for entrepreneurship and the rehabilitation of honest debtors. Court confirmation of plans and the liquidation-floor test protect minorities against coerced restructuring. The accepted trade-off is moral hazard, contained here by the fraud and willful-injury exclusions from discharge.*
