# Section 0.2 — Constitutional Principles

> **Part 00 — Foundation** · Articles 0.2.1 – 0.2.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section declares the founding principles of the Republic and gives them binding force for all public power.*

## Article 0.2.1 — Constitutionalism

1. The Republic is founded on constitutionalism. Every public power is created by, bound by, and answerable to this Constitution.
2. No organ of the State, and no person exercising public power, may act without a legal basis or beyond the limits this Constitution imposes.
3. These principles bind all organs of the State and inform the interpretation of every other provision.

## Article 0.2.2 — Democratic Self-Government

1. The people govern themselves through free elections, through their representatives in the Parliament, through local government, and through the forms of participation this Constitution provides.
2. Elections shall be free, periodic, and fair, and administered without interference.
3. Every person has the right to take part in the public affairs of the Republic, directly or through freely chosen representatives.

## Article 0.2.3 — Pluralism

1. The Republic recognizes pluralism in opinion, belief, culture, and language as a source of democratic vitality and protects it as such.
2. Public institutions shall serve all persons equally and shall remain independent of every party and interest group.
3. The diversity of the people shall be reflected in public life.

## Article 0.2.4 — Protection of Minorities

1. Persons belonging to minorities shall be protected in their identity, language, culture, and religion, and shall not be forced to assimilate.
2. Measures may secure the effective participation of minorities in public life, consistent with Article 1.1.4 (Limitation of Rights).
3. The presence or absence of a numerical majority shall not determine the scope of any right.

## Article 0.2.5 — Intergenerational Responsibility

1. The natural environment, the public finances, and the cultural heritage of the Republic are held in trust for present and future generations.
2. Public decisions shall take account of their long-term consequences; no generation shall foreclose the ability of the next to meet its own needs.
3. Implementation belongs to the Parts governing the environment, public finance, and social policy.

## Article 0.2.6 — Application of the Principles

1. The principles in this Section are binding norms, not aspirations.
2. Courts and every interpreter shall apply them in resolving uncertainty in the meaning of any provision of this Constitution or of any law.
3. Nothing in this Section confers a power to override an express provision of this Constitution.

## Design Note

*This Section gathers the Republic's founding commitments in one place so that later Parts can invoke a stable interpretive anchor, a technique favored by modern constitutions that state founding values before their rights catalogues. The principles carry interpretive and binding force, yet cannot contradict express text, which preserves clarity about what is command and what is guidance. Minority protection is paired with pluralism, treating diversity as an affirmative constitutional asset rather than a problem to be contained.*
