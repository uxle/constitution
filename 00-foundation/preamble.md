# Section 0.1 — Preamble

> **Part 00 — Foundation** · Preamble · Adopted — v1.0.0

*The Preamble proclaims the identity, values, and founding resolve of the Republic and records the adoption of this Constitution.*

We the people of the Republic, remembering the struggles by which our freedom was won, grateful for the inheritance we hold in trust, and resolved to govern ourselves under law rather than force, do ordain and adopt this Constitution as the supreme law of our common life.

We affirm that human dignity is inviolable; that all public authority belongs to the people; and that the State exists to serve the person, never the person the State. We establish a unitary, democratic republic in which the law binds governor and governed alike, in which power is divided and checked so that no organ may rule alone, and in which the independence of courts and the integrity of public institutions are secure.

We commit ourselves to the rule of law; to equality before the law without discrimination of any kind; to pluralism in opinion, belief, and culture, with special care for minorities; to representative, participatory, and direct democracy through free and periodic elections; to freedom of expression and of the media; to solidarity among all who live here, including those who seek refuge among us; and to the protection of the natural and cultural inheritance of the Republic for those who come after us.

We acknowledge that rights are inseparable from duties. Every person shall respect the dignity and the rights of others; every organ of the State shall exercise its powers honestly, transparently, and accountably; and prosperity today shall not be purchased at the expense of tomorrow.

This Constitution is adopted by the people in a national referendum, having been deliberated upon and enacted by a freely elected constituent assembly, and it enters into force upon the proclamation of its ratification. From that moment, all public power flows from this Constitution alone, and any law or act inconsistent with it is without effect to the extent of the inconsistency.
