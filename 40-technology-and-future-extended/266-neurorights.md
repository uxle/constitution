# WC-266 · NeuroRights

> **Part 40 — Technology and Future Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 266/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Mental privacy, cognitive liberty and personality protection

## Preamble

This document protects the mind against emerging neurotechnology.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Mental privacy

1.1 Neural data are the most protected category of personal data; collection requires explicit consent for narrow purposes.

1.2 Real-time decoding of thought or affect without consent is prohibited.

1.3 Consumer neurotech follows medical-grade privacy rules regardless of marketing category.

### Article 2 — Cognitive liberty

2.1 No person shall be subjected to neuro-interventions without free informed consent - non-consensual alteration of brain function is a crime against bodily and mental integrity.

2.2 Coerced consent in custody, employment and education is void; neuro-interrogation is torture under document 121.

2.3 Military and security neuro-applications follow the prohibition - no exception.

### Article 3 — Personality continuity

3.1 Interventions materially affecting personality, memory or agency require ethics review, medical indication and reversibility assessment.

3.2 Enhancement by free choice is lawful with safety regulation; workplace coercion of enhancement is prohibited.

3.3 The integrity of the person is not negotiable by contract.

### Article 4 — Institutional

4.1 The Technology Authority's register includes neuro systems; the bioethics council reviews protocols.

4.2 Neurorights are included in the unamendable core review list of document 008's dignity clause.

4.3 Research follows the 3Rs adapted to sentience -Replacement, Reduction, Refinement plus welfare.

## Commentary

Neurights are constitutionalised before the technology matures because the mind is the last inviolable sphere - waiting for the market to mature first is how every privacy battle was lost. The consent absolutism covers the coercion channels - custody, work, school - where neuro-tools would otherwise arrive as conditions. Placing mental integrity under the dignity clause means future devices meet the corpus's oldest protection on day one.
