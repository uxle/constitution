# WC-270 · The Future Generations Council

> **Part 40 — Technology and Future Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 270/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** A standing voice for those not yet born

## Preamble

This document establishes and arms the Future Generations Council.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Composition

1.1 The Council has eleven members: four by civic lottery among citizens under thirty, four by open competition for expertise in ecology, economics, health and technology, and three nominated by civil society.

1.2 Members serve staggered five-year terms with the protections of constitutional organs under document 075.

1.3 Youth members rotate every two years.

### Article 2 — Powers

2.1 The Council reviews all bills, budgets and national strategies for generational impact and issues published opinions.

2.2 Opinions attach to parliamentary documents; the responsible committee must answer in writing before final vote - the binding answer duty.

2.3 The Council may petition the Constitutional Court on breaches of the sustainability principles of documents 003 and 046.

### Article 3 — Instruments

3.1 The Council maintains the generational accounts, the long-term risk register and the twenty-five-year state of the future report.

3.2 It runs annual youth assemblies feeding its agenda.

3.3 Its secretariat is professional and its data follows document 258's commons.

### Article 4 — Guarantees

4.1 The Council's budget is protected like other constitutional organs.

4.2 Abolition or weakening follows the amendment procedure with the entrenched protection of document 009.

4.3 Government departments must respond to Council information requests within thirty days.

## Commentary

The Council is the enforcement organ of document 046: representation with procedure - published opinions, binding answers, generational accounts and standing before the constitutional court. Lottery seats for the young make the institution demographically honest about whose future it defends. The Welsh pioneer proved the model works; the corpus arms it with justiciability the Welsh commissioner lacked.
