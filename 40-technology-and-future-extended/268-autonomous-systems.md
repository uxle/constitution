# WC-268 · Autonomous Systems

> **Part 11 — Technology and the Future** · Document **268 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Robots, drones and vehicles: responsibility without gaps

## Preamble

This document governs autonomous physical systems and their liability.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Safety and approval

1.1 High-risk autonomous systems - vehicles, drones, industrial robots - require conformity approval with safety cases and scenario testing.

1.2 Autonomous weapons that select and engage targets without human control are prohibited absolutely.

1.3 Public-space operation requires registration, identification and insurance.

### Article 2 — Human oversight

2.1 Safety-critical autonomous operations retain human supervisory control with takeover capacity proportionate to risk.

2.2 High-consequence civilian uses - policing, care, transport of persons - keep meaningful human control under document 256.

2.3 Autonomy levels are disclosed to users and passengers.

### Article 3 — Liability

3.1 Liability for harm by autonomous systems is strict for operators and manufacturers with reversed burden on defect for victims.

3.2 Black-box logging is mandatory for accident reconstruction with privacy-preserving access rules.

3.3 No liability gaps: contracts cannot shift responsibility away from accountable parties.

### Article 4 — Labour and ethics

4.1 Workplace automation follows consultation duties and transition guarantees of documents 256 and 205.

4.2 Care robots assist, never replace, human care relationships in elder and child care.

4.3 Research ethics reviews cover human-robot interaction studies.

## Commentary

Autonomous systems arrive with a responsibility vacuum - the vendor blames the user, the user the vendor - so the chapter closes it: strict liability, reversed burden, black boxes for truth. The autonomous-weapons prohibition follows the majority international position and the corpus's human-responsibility spine. The care-robot clause keeps technology in service of the care relationship rather than as its cheap replacement.

## Notes for adopting states

- The technology chapters presume the data-protection framework; sequence document 026's statute before AI and platform rules.
- The algorithmic register can start small - every public body lists its five most consequential systems in year one.
- Digital-identity rollout must include the offline fallbacks from day one or the excluded will litigate rightly.
