# WC-260 · Digital Infrastructure as Public Utility

> **Part 40 — Technology and Future Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 260/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Connectivity, compute and platforms as essential infrastructure

## Preamble

This document governs digital infrastructure with utility discipline.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Connectivity

1.1 Universal high-speed connectivity is a statutory obligation with geographic and social tariffs under document 026's access right.

1.2 Backbone and passive infrastructure are open-access wholesale; retail competition rides on it.

1.3 Network resilience and outage compensation follow licence conditions.

### Article 2 — Compute and clouds

2.1 Strategic compute capacity is public or mixed-ownership with open access for research and SMEs.

2.2 Public workloads avoid single-vendor lock-in under document 170; data portability is contractual in every public cloud.

2.3 Sovereign hosting for critical public systems follows document 242's defence requirements.

### Article 3 — Platforms

3.1 Gatekeeper platforms follow document 188's duties: interoperability, portability, fair terms.

3.2 Essential private services - payments, app stores, messaging - carry continuity obligations like utilities.

3.3 Marketplace dominance is regulated, not sanctified.

### Article 4 — Funding

4.1 Universal service funds finance unprofitable areas transparently with open-data coverage maps.

4.2 Infrastructure sharing rules cut civil-works costs; permit clocks under document 166 accelerate builds.

4.3 Public digital investment follows the appraisal gates of document 200.

## Commentary

Connectivity became the condition of citizenship - taxes, school, health - so the chapter treats it with utility law's old virtues: universal obligation, open wholesale, continuity duties. Public compute answers the market's structural concentration in the AI era with access rather than prohibition. Open-data coverage maps keep subsidy honesty visible to the towns the maps forgot.
