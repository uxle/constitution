# WC-265 · Genetic and Reproductive Rights

> **Part 11 — Technology and the Future** · Document **265 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Your genes are yours: privacy, non-discrimination, choice

## Preamble

This document protects persons in their genetic and reproductive dimensions.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Genetic privacy

1.1 Genetic data are special-category data under document 026: explicit consent for each purpose, no surreptitious testing.

1.2 Genetic test results belong to the person; relatives' access follows consent or narrow judicial order.

1.3 Genetic biobanks operate under the commons rules of document 258 with withdrawal rights.

### Article 2 — Non-discrimination

2.1 Genetic features are a prohibited discrimination ground under document 016 for insurance, employment, credit and education.

2.2 Insurers may not require or use predictive genetic results - a bright-line rule.

2.3 Employers may test only for demonstrated safety-relevant conditions with consent.

### Article 3 — Reproductive choice

3.1 Reproductive decisions belong to the person: contraception, fertility treatment including IVF and safe abortion services are health rights under document 018.

3.2 Pre-implantation testing is restricted to serious conditions with counselling; sex selection for non-medical reasons is prohibited.

3.3 Surrogacy is regulated for altruistic forms with the child's and surrogate's rights protected; commercial exploitation is prohibited.

### Article 4 — Parentage and identity

4.1 Persons have the right to know their genetic origins with donor-identity rules balancing donors' privacy.

4.2 Genetic parentage tests follow court order or consent - secret tests are inadmissible and sanctionable.

4.3 Adoptees and donor-conceived persons access origin information at majority.

## Commentary

Genetic information is the most intimate and the most familial data, so the chapter fixes bright lines: no insurer demands, no employer fishing, no secret testing. Reproductive rights are treated as health rights within document 018's guarantees - the frame that survived constitutional review in the strongest systems. Origin-information rights answer the adult wounds that secrecy regimes produced.
