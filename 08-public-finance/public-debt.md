# Section 8.3 — Public Debt

> **Part 08 — Public Finance** · Articles 8.3.1 – 8.3.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section governs public borrowing and debt: statutory authorization, a published debt strategy, sustainability limits fixed by law, and the prohibition of monetary financing.*

## Article 8.3.1 — Statutory Authorization

1. The Republic may borrow, guarantee, or assume liabilities only by or under a statute that fixes the purpose and the ceiling.
2. An authorization shall state the maximum principal and the purposes for which proceeds may be used.
3. Guarantees and assumed liabilities shall be recorded and shall count toward the sustainability limits fixed by law.

## Article 8.3.2 — Debt Strategy and Reporting

1. The Government shall publish a medium-term debt strategy before each fiscal year and shall report annually on its execution.
2. A consolidated register of the debt, guarantees, and contingent liabilities of the State shall be maintained and published.
3. The Parliament shall be informed without delay of borrowing under standing authorizations.

## Article 8.3.3 — Sustainability Limits

1. The law shall fix reference values for the annual deficit and for total public debt, consistent with Article 8.7.1 (Principle of Sustainability).
2. Where a reference value is exceeded, the Government shall present a corrective plan, and the Parliament shall take the decisions it requires.
3. Escape clauses for exceptional events shall be defined by law, narrow in scope, and subject to reporting and a defined return path within the reference values.

## Article 8.3.4 — Prohibition of Monetary Financing

1. The State shall not obtain credit from the Central Bank, and the Central Bank shall not purchase debt instruments of the State at primary issuance.
2. Overdraft facilities of the Treasury with the Central Bank are prohibited.
3. Narrow operations of the Central Bank for monetary policy purposes are governed by Article 8.4.4 (Instruments and Financial Stability) and do not constitute monetary financing.

## Design Note

*Borrowing is taxation postponed, so it is subjected to the same parliamentary authorship as taxation itself. A published strategy and a consolidated register of liabilities, including guarantees, make the true debt visible before elections rather than after. The monetary-financing prohibition protects the currency from fiscal dominance, while the narrow exception in Section 8.4 keeps ordinary monetary operations lawful without giving the State a hidden lender.*
