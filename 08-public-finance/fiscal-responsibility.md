# Section 8.7 — Fiscal Responsibility

> **Part 08 — Public Finance** · Articles 8.7.1 – 8.7.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section imposes the duties of fiscal responsibility: intergenerational sustainability, countercyclical management, reference targets fixed by law, an independent fiscal council, and risk reporting.*

## Article 8.7.1 — Principle of Sustainability

1. Fiscal policy shall be conducted so that present financing needs do not shift unreasonable burdens to future generations.
2. In periods of exceptional revenue, the Republic shall build reserves or reduce debt, and countercyclical buffers shall be maintained so that downturns need not be met by procyclical retrenchment.
3. Windfall revenues from non-renewable natural resources shall be treated under the fund regime of Part 13 (Environment and Natural Resources).

## Article 8.7.2 — Reference Targets

1. The law shall fix reference values for the deficit and for the debt of the Republic, consistent with Article 8.3.3 (Sustainability Limits).
2. Budgets shall be based on prudent and realistic macroeconomic and revenue assumptions.
3. Deviation from a reference value requires a published justification and a defined return path.

## Article 8.7.3 — Independent Fiscal Council

1. There shall be an independent fiscal council which assesses macroeconomic forecasts, compliance with the reference values, and long-term fiscal sustainability.
2. The council shall have unrestricted access to the data of the public administration and shall publish its assessments.
3. The assessment of the council on the draft budget shall be laid before the Parliament before adoption.

## Article 8.7.4 — Fiscal Risk Reporting

1. The budget documents shall include a statement of fiscal risks, including contingent liabilities, guarantees, and the finances of public entities.
2. The Government shall update the statement during the year where a material risk crystallizes.
3. The statement shall distinguish risks that can be absorbed within existing appropriations from those requiring a parliamentary decision.

## Design Note

*Intergenerational equity is given operational content through buffers and reference targets rather than through a single numerical golden rule, because the right number varies with the economy and the era. An independent fiscal council corrects the documented optimism bias of government forecasts at the moment it matters most, before the budget is adopted. Risk reporting extends visibility to the guarantees and contingent liabilities where hidden debt historically accumulates.*
