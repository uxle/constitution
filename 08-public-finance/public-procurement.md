# Section 8.5 — Public Procurement

> **Part 08 — Public Finance** · Articles 8.5.1 – 8.5.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section governs public procurement: open competitive procedures, value for money, integrity, an independent review body, and transparency through electronic publication.*

## Article 8.5.1 — Principles

1. Public procurement shall be open, competitive, non-discriminatory, and transparent, and shall achieve value for money.
2. Award shall follow equal treatment of bidders, proportionality, and the protection of competition under Article 9.2.1 (Protection of Competition).
3. Procurement shall not be used to grant favors to political associates or to distort competition.

## Article 8.5.2 — Procedures and Award Criteria

1. Contracts above the thresholds fixed by law shall be awarded after public advertisement of the opportunity, the criteria, and the procedure.
2. Award criteria shall be fixed in advance and applied as published; the decision shall be reasoned and notified to the bidders.
3. Direct award shall be permitted only in the narrow cases fixed by law, shall be documented, and shall be reported.

## Article 8.5.3 — Integrity Rules

1. Persons involved in procurement shall declare conflicts of interest and shall withdraw where their impartiality may be compromised.
2. Bidders who have engaged in corruption, fraud, or collusion may be excluded from procedures for the period fixed by law, after a decision subject to review.
3. The splitting of contracts to evade thresholds is prohibited.

## Article 8.5.4 — Independent Review Body

1. An independent review body shall hear the complaints of bidders concerning the conduct and the outcome of procedures.
2. It may order the correction of the procedure, the suspension of an award, or the annulment of a decision, and may impose the sanctions fixed by law.
3. Its decisions shall be reasoned, published in anonymized form, and subject to appeal to the courts.

## Article 8.5.5 — E-Procurement and Publication

1. The law shall provide for an electronic procurement system as the ordinary channel for advertising, submission, and record-keeping.
2. Notices, award decisions, contract values, and, for significant contracts, performance reports shall be published in open data form.
3. The records of procedures shall be retained for audit under Article 8.8.2 (Mandate).

## Design Note

*Competition for public money is the cheapest anti-corruption technology available to the State, which is why openness and advertised criteria come before any efficiency exception. An independent review body gives losing bidders a fast, expert remedy that ordinary courts alone cannot match in speed, while judicial appeal remains intact. Publishing contract values in open data lets journalists and competitors do the oversight that inspectors cannot scale.*
