# Section 8.2 — The Budget

> **Part 08 — Public Finance** · Articles 8.2.1 – 8.2.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section governs the annual budget: preparation and unity, parliamentary adoption before the fiscal year, transparency, and the contingency reserve.*

## Article 8.2.1 — The Annual Budget Law

1. All revenues and expenditures of the Republic for each fiscal year shall be fixed by the annual budget law, which the Parliament shall adopt before the fiscal year begins.
2. The Government shall submit the draft budget to the Lower House in sufficient time for its adoption before the fiscal year begins.
3. Multi-year expenditure shall be stated separately in the budget law.

## Article 8.2.2 — Unity and Completeness

1. The budget shall comprise all revenues and all expenditures of the State, without offsetting and without funds outside the budget.
2. Revenues shall not be earmarked except as provided by law for specific funds subject to the audit of Article 8.8.2 (Mandate).
3. The budget shall be presented in a structure that permits informed parliamentary decision.

## Article 8.2.3 — Parliamentary Adoption

1. The Parliament holds primacy over appropriations; it may amend the draft budget, reducing expenditure freely and increasing it only where offset by corresponding revenue or savings.
2. A money bill follows the procedure of Article 3.3.5 (Powers over Money Bills), including the limited delay applicable to money bills.
3. Where the budget is not adopted before the fiscal year begins, the Lower House may by law authorize continuation of prior appropriations for a strictly limited period; no new programs may be started under such authority.

## Article 8.2.4 — Budget Transparency

1. The draft budget, its supporting documents, and its macroeconomic assumptions shall be published upon submission.
2. The Parliament shall hold public hearings on the draft budget.
3. The adopted budget, its execution, and the final accounts shall be published in open data form.

## Article 8.2.5 — Contingency Reserve

1. The budget law may establish a contingency reserve for unforeseeable and unavoidable needs.
2. Drawings on the reserve shall be reported to the Parliament without delay and shall be approved by the Parliament after the fact within the period fixed by law.

## Design Note

*The deadline — adopted before the fiscal year — is treated as a constitutional rule rather than an aspiration, because the interim-continuation clause is deliberately narrow: no new programs and a strict time limit. Parliamentary primacy over appropriations includes the power to amend, with offsetting requirements preserving overall balance. A contingency reserve recognizes that no parliament can foresee everything, while ex-post approval keeps even surprises inside the constitutional circuit of authorization.*

**Cross-references:** Article 3.3.5 (Powers over Money Bills); Article 8.6.1 (No Expenditure Without Authorization); Article 8.8.2 (Mandate).
