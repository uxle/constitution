# Definitions

Operative defined terms used normatively throughout this Constitution. Wherever these terms appear capitalized in a section file, they carry the meaning stated here. Each entry states the meaning and the section in which the term is established or developed. Interpretive rules for the Constitution itself are found in Section 25.3.

## The State and its Territory

1. **"the Republic"** means the sovereign, unitary, democratic state constituted by this Constitution, acting through its organs and on the whole of its territory (Sections 0.1–0.3).
2. **"the State"** means the Republic as a legal person, including all organs of state at every level (Section 0.3).
3. **"the territory of the Republic"** means the land, waters, and airspace over which the Republic exercises sovereignty, as defined by law (Section 18.6).
4. **"organ of state"** means any department, institution, court, tribunal, body, or functionary exercising public power or performing a public function under the Constitution or law (Section 29.2).
5. **"public authority"** means any organ of state and any private person exercising public power or performing a public function under law (Section 29.2).

## The People

6. **"the people of the Republic"** means the community of citizens and all persons subject to the Republic's laws, in whom sovereignty resides (Sections 0.3, 2.1).
7. **"citizen"** means a person holding the citizenship of the Republic by birth, descent, registration, or naturalization (Section 2.1).
8. **"non-citizen"** means any person present in the territory of the Republic who is not a citizen, whose lawful residence confers rights that may not be arbitrarily withheld (Sections 2.3–2.5).
9. **"refugee"** means a person recognized under Section 2.5 as owing to a well-founded fear of persecution and unable or unwilling to avail themselves of the protection of their State (Section 2.5).

## Constitutional Fundamentals

10. **"human dignity"** means the inviolable intrinsic worth of every person, the supreme value from which all rights derive (Section 0.7).
11. **"the rule of law"** means the requirement that all public power derive from, be exercised under, and conform to law that is public, clear, general, prospective, and equally enforced (Section 0.4).
12. **"constitutional supremacy"** means the obligation of every organ and person to accord with this Constitution, which prevails over inconsistent law and conduct (Section 0.8).
13. **"entrenched provision"** means a provision listed in Section 30.5, amendable only under Section 25.1 by two-thirds of each House plus a national referendum (Sections 25.1, 30.5).
14. **"non-derogable right"** means a right listed in Section 1.1 and Section 26.5 that may not be limited by derogation during a state of emergency (Sections 1.1, 26.5).

## Rights Doctrine

15. **"right"** means an entitlement conferred or recognized by Part 01, enforceable by any person to whom it applies (Section 1.1).
16. **"limitation of a right"** means any measure that restricts, burdens, or otherwise interferes with the enjoyment of a right, lawful only under Article 1.1.4 (Section 1.1).
17. **"proportionality"** means the analytical sequence of legitimacy of aim, suitability, necessity, and balance stricto sensu, applied to every limitation (Article 1.1.4).
18. **"derogation"** means a temporary, published, parliamentary-approved suspension of a derogable right during a state of emergency (Section 26.5).

## The Legislature

19. **"the Parliament"** means the bicameral legislature of the Republic, consisting of the Lower House and the Upper House (Section 3.1).
20. **"the Lower House"** means the chamber of four hundred members elected by proportional representation for four-year terms (Section 3.2).
21. **"the Upper House"** means the chamber of Provincial representation, three members per Province elected by the Provincial Councils for staggered six-year terms (Section 3.3).
22. **"Act"** means a law duly adopted by Parliament and assented to under Section 3.8 (Section 3.8).
23. **"money bill"** means a bill dealing only with taxation, appropriation, public debt, or the budget, subject to the special procedure of Section 3.3 (Sections 3.3, 8.2).

## The Executive

24. **"the President"** means the head of state, elected by a joint sitting of Parliament for a single six-year term (Section 4.1).
25. **"the Prime Minister"** means the head of government, a member of the Lower House who commands its confidence (Section 4.2).
26. **"the Cabinet"** means the Prime Minister and the ministers, acting under collective responsibility (Section 4.3).
27. **"the Public Service"** means the career body of officials serving the executive under merit and neutrality principles (Section 4.5).
28. **"countersignature"** means the ministerial signature required for the validity of specified presidential acts (Sections 4.1, 26.1).

## The Judiciary and Prosecution

29. **"the Constitutional Court"** means the apex court for constitutional questions, of eleven judges serving single non-renewable twelve-year terms (Section 5.3).
30. **"the Supreme Court"** means the apex court of the general court system, deciding final appeals and unifying case law (Section 5.2).
31. **"the Judicial Service Commission"** means the independent body whose merit-based recommendations bind the President in judicial appointments (Section 5.5).
32. **"the Public Prosecution Service"** means the independent institution responsible for criminal prosecutions, headed by the Public Prosecutor (Section 5.8).
33. **"judicial review"** means the power described in Section 5.6 to refuse application to, or invalidate, unconstitutional acts (Section 5.6).

## Subnational Government

34. **"Province"** means a primary subnational unit of the Republic governed by an elected Provincial Council (Section 7.1).
35. **"Region"** means a cooperative association of Provinces formed for shared services, without separate sovereignty (Section 7.2).
36. **"Municipality"** means a local-government unit governed by an elected Municipal Council (Section 7.3).
37. **"subsidiarity"** means the allocation of functions to the lowest level of government capable of discharging them effectively (Section 7.6).

## Public Money

38. **"the Central Bank"** means the independent monetary authority with price stability as its primary mandate (Section 8.4).
39. **"the Office of the Auditor-General"** means the independent institution auditing all public funds and reporting to Parliament (Sections 8.8, 20.5).
40. **"the national currency"** means the unit of account of the Republic, whose name and denominations are fixed by law (Section 8.4).
41. **"public funds"** means all revenues, borrowings, and other resources received or controlled by any organ of state (Section 8.6).

## Elections and Emergency

42. **"the Electoral Commission"** means the independent institution administering all national elections and referendums (Sections 3.5, 20.4).
43. **"state of emergency"** means the crisis regime declared under Section 26.1, subject to parliamentary approval, time limits, and judicial review (Section 26.1).
44. **"entrenchment"** means the heightened amendment protection attaching to provisions listed in Section 30.5 (Section 30.5).

## Independent Institutions

45. **"independent institution"** means a constitutional organ listed in Part 20 — the Ombudsman, the Human Rights Commission, the Anti-Corruption Commission, the Electoral Commission, the Office of the Auditor-General, the Statistics Authority, and the Information Commission — characterized by security of tenure, protected budgets, and removal only for cause (Part 20).
46. **"the Information Commission"** means the independent institution supervising both data protection and access to information (Sections 17.2, 20.7, 27.2).
