# Section 26.2 — Natural Disasters

> **Part 26 — Emergency and Crisis** · Articles 26.2.1 – 26.2.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section organizes disaster response through a standing management authority, a solidarity fund, limited evacuation powers, and binding reconstruction duties.*

## Article 26.2.1 — Disaster Management Authority

1. An Act shall establish a national disaster-management authority for prevention, preparedness, response, and recovery.
2. The authority shall operate continuously, and not only during a state of emergency under Section 26.1.
3. The authority shall coordinate the services of the State, the Provinces, and the Municipalities under Article 7.6.1 (The Subsidiarity Principle).
4. The authority may exercise coercive powers only where this Constitution or an Act confers them.

## Article 26.2.2 — The Solidarity Fund

1. An Act shall establish a national solidarity fund for relief and reconstruction.
2. The fund shall be financed from appropriations, lawful donations, and other sources fixed by law.
3. Allocations shall follow published criteria, shall be reported publicly, and shall prioritize the persons and communities most severely affected.
4. The accounts of the fund shall be audited under Article 8.8.1 (Scope of Audit).

## Article 26.2.3 — Evacuation and Urgent Measures

1. An evacuation may be ordered only where necessary to protect life or health, only for the shortest necessary duration, and only by the authority or minister designated by law.
2. An evacuation order shall be reasoned in writing and shall be subject to prompt review by a court.
3. Public authorities shall protect the property of evacuated persons and shall prevent unlawful entry into evacuated areas.
4. Nothing in this Article authorizes expropriation except under Article 1.8.2 (Expropriation).

## Article 26.2.4 — Reconstruction

1. Public authorities shall restore essential services, housing, and infrastructure within the shortest reasonable time.
2. Reconstruction shall prioritize the persons and areas most severely affected and shall apply resilience standards that reduce future vulnerability.
3. Contracts for reconstruction shall be awarded under Article 8.5.1 (Principles) and published.

## Article 26.2.5 — Preparedness and Early Warning

1. The State shall maintain:
   (a) risk assessments for foreseeable disasters;
   (b) early-warning systems; and
   (c) public information duties.
2. Periodic exercises shall test the emergency plans of every level of government.
3. Operators of critical infrastructure shall cooperate with the authority under the resilience regime of Part 23 (Infrastructure).

## Design Note

*Disaster response is separated from the state of emergency because catastrophes demand logistics rather than rights restrictions, and only the narrow evacuation power touches liberty. The solidarity fund constitutionalizes shared-cost relief, while routing its accounts through the supreme audit institution keeps crisis spending from becoming patronage. The reconstruction duty ensures the regime does not end at the evacuation, and subsidiarity keeps local first responders in the lead.*
