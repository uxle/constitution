# Section 18.6 — Border Security

> **Part 18 — National Security and Defense** · Articles 18.6.1 – 18.6.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section requires lawful and humane border management, entrenches the absolute prohibition of refoulement, and confines border searches and surveillance to proportionate, legally grounded measures.*

## Article 18.6.1 — Lawful and Humane Border Management

1. Border control shall be exercised by designated civilian authorities on the basis of law and with respect for human dignity.
2. Measures taken at borders shall be suitable, necessary, and proportionate to their declared purpose.
3. The State shall ensure at the borders the same respect for rights that binds every organ of the Republic within the territory.

## Article 18.6.2 — Absolute Non-Refoulement

1. No person shall be refouled, at a border or elsewhere, to a territory where they face persecution, torture, or other serious irreparable harm.
2. This prohibition is absolute; no emergency, security interest, or agreement of the Republic shall displace it.
3. A person who expresses fear of such harm shall have access to the asylum procedure under Article 2.4.1 before any removal, consistent with Article 2.4.2.

## Article 18.6.3 — Proportionate Border Searches

1. Searches of persons, vehicles, goods, and electronic devices at borders require a legal basis and individualized justification; sweeping routine examination of devices is prohibited.
2. Examination of an electronic device requires judicial authorization save in urgent cases confirmed retrospectively; only data relevant to the lawful purpose may be retained.
3. Persons in border procedures shall be informed of their rights in a language they understand and shall have access to interpretation.

## Article 18.6.4 — Border Surveillance and Data

1. Border surveillance shall be targeted on lawful objectives; generalized monitoring of persons without individualized suspicion is prohibited.
2. Data collected at borders shall be used only for lawful border management, retained no longer than necessary, and protected in accordance with Article 1.5.4.

## Design Note

*Non-refoulement is stated here without exception because sending a person to torture or persecution destroys the very rights the border is meant to secure, a reading consistent with the absolute character of the underlying prohibitions. Border searches receive a stricter rule for electronic devices, whose contents exceed anything a traveler's bag once held. Channeling all border coercion through civilian authorities and ordinary proportionality review prevents the frontier from becoming a rights-free zone.*
