# WC-241 · Arms Control and Disarmament

> **Part 39 — Security and International Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 241/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Weapons of mass destruction banned, conventional trade controlled

## Preamble

This document implements the disarmament commitments of the state.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Weapons of mass destruction

1.1 Nuclear, biological and chemical weapons and their means of delivery are prohibited; their acquisition, development, stockpiling and transfer are crimes under document 139.

1.2 The state joins and implements all relevant treaties - NPT, CWC, BWC, TPNW.

1.3 Any treaty-compatible civilian nuclear programme is under international safeguards with domestic transparency.

### Article 2 — Conventional arms

2.1 Arms exports follow humanitarian criteria: no exports where they would violate humanitarian law or human rights, or to conflict parties; embargo violations are crimes.

2.2 End-user certificates, brokering controls and diversion risk assessments are statutory.

2.3 Arms export decisions above thresholds require parliamentary committee notification with annual reports.

### Article 3 — Domestic

3.1 Civilian firearms licensing is strict: background checks, training, registration and safe storage; automatic weapons are prohibited for civilians.

3.2 Ammunition marking enables tracing; 3D-printed ghost guns are treated as firearms.

3.3 Buyback and amnesty programmes operate periodically with public safety evaluation.

### Article 4 — Disarmament diplomacy

4.1 The state supports disarmament negotiations, verification science and transparency measures.

4.2 Defence conversion programmes assist industry transition under document 205's machinery.

4.3 Arms-trade corruption follows document 108 with priority - a documented epicentre of grand corruption.

## Commentary

Arms corruption is grand corruption's veteran form: commissions on weapon deals funded party systems across the democratic world, so the chapter inserts the integrity machinery directly into export control. The domestic licensing follows the systems with the lowest gun death rates - registration, training, storage. Defence conversion funding keeps the disarmament commitment honest for workers who built the old arsenal.
