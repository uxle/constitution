# WC-252 · Peacekeeping and Peacebuilding

> **Part 39 — Security and International Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 252/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Blue helmets and the long peace: consent, protection, accountability

## Preamble

This document governs participation in international peace operations.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Participation

1.1 Contributions to UN, regional and mandated operations follow document 236's authorisation with mission definitions.

1.2 Troop-contributing decisions state protection-of-civilians mandates explicitly.

1.3 No participation in operations without adequate legal protection frameworks.

### Article 2 — Conduct

2.1 National jurisdiction over personnel for crimes applies extraterritorially; immunity agreements never cover crimes.

2.2 Sexual exploitation and abuse are prosecuted with victim-first procedure; zero tolerance means zero exceptions in law.

2.3 Mission-wide conduct data publish with incident statistics.

### Article 3 — Peacebuilding

3.1 Post-conflict programmes follow rule-of-law first: courts, integrity institutions and records before elections.

3.2 Disarmament, demobilisation and reintegration are funded as decade programmes, not photo-ops.

3.3 Local ownership: national actors lead with international support under documented compacts.

### Article 4 — Learning

4.1 Operation lessons publish within two years of completion.

4.2 Casualties of local staff and civilians are counted and reported - no invisible victims.

4.3 Peace-operation expenditure follows document 098 with mission-level audits.

## Commentary

Peace operations carry the UN's finest purposes and its worst scandals, and both trace to accountability: the chapter keeps national criminal jurisdiction extraterritorial so immunity never shields abuse. Rule-of-law-first peacebuilding follows the learned sequence of every successful transition - honest courts before competitive elections. Counting local victims and staff honours those the statistics historically erased.
