# WC-251 · Human Rights in Foreign Policy and Sanctions

> **Part 10 — Security and International Order** · Document **251 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Global Magnitsky machinery, arms criteria, honest trade-offs

## Preamble

This document implements human-rights and anti-corruption sanctions and coherence.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Sanctions

1.1 Targeted sanctions - asset freezes and entry bans - are imposed on individuals responsible for gross violations and significant corruption by statutory process.

1.2 Designations are evidence-based, with due-process notice, challenge and delisting before the sanctions court.

1.3 Listings coordinate with allies and publish reasoned summaries.

### Article 2 — Coherence

2.1 Arms exports apply document 239's humanitarian criteria; human-rights dialogues carry deliverables and consequences.

2.2 Development and trade preferences are tied to rights and anti-corruption performance with published scorecards.

2.3 No torture-rendition cooperation: intelligence-sharing excludes torture-tainted information.

### Article 3 — Protection

3.1 Human-rights defenders abroad receive visa fast-tracks, emergency shelter and asylum priority under document 028.

3.2 Journalists at risk receive protective visas and relocation support.

3.3 Election observation is offered and received as good practice under document 052.

### Article 4 — Honesty

4.1 The government reports annually to Parliament on rights dialogues, sanctions imposed and evaded, and coherence failures.

4.2 Commercial interests never silently override the criteria; overrides are stated and justified publicly.

4.3 Victims participate in sanctions design through consultation.

## Commentary

Global human-rights machinery works only when it is lawful, targeted and honest, which is why the chapter adds due process and delisting to the Magnitsky instruments - legitimacy is the sanction's fuel. The coherence clauses prevent the documented pattern of values speeches followed by arms deliveries. Emergency visas for defenders and journalists are the cheapest and most honourable foreign policy there is.
