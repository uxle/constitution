# WC-243 · Civil Protection and Disaster Management

> **Part 39 — Security and International Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 243/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Preparedness, response and honest recovery

## Preamble

This document governs protection against disasters natural and man-made.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Preparedness

1.1 A national civil-protection agency maintains hazard and risk maps, scenario plans and stockpiles.

1.2 Critical infrastructure operators follow resilience standards, stress tests and outage reporting under document 261.

1.3 Regular public exercises include communities and schools.

### Article 2 — Response

2.1 Response is coordinated by the agency with clear command under document 080's emergency declarations.

2.2 Volunteers and NGOs integrate through pre-agreed frameworks with equipment and insurance.

2.3 Public communication is single-voiced, honest about uncertainty and multilingual.

### Article 3 — Recovery

3.1 Relief aid distribution follows transparency rules: beneficiary lists anonymised, procurements under document 098's emergency caps.

3.2 Insurance pools and solidarity funds are pre-financed by statute with triggers.

3.3 Reconstruction builds back to higher resilience standards.

### Article 4 — Learning

4.1 Every major event triggers an independent review with a public report.

4.2 Recommendations are tracked in the follow-up regime of document 077.

4.3 Compensation for state failure in duty of care follows ordinary liability.

## Commentary

Disaster corruption is a documented genre - relief diverted, reconstruction bid-rigged - so the chapter applies the full integrity machinery to the emergency window rather than exempting it. Pre-financed solidarity funds with automatic triggers replace the discretionary begging that characterised catastrophic recovery. Honest-uncertainty communication is mandated because false reassurance is the deadliest product of disaster politics.

## Notes for adopting states

- War powers and intelligence warrants require the surveillance court; build it first.
