# Section 32.1 — Animal Welfare Baseline

## Articles

**Article 32.1.1 (Sentience Recognized).** Animals are recognized as sentient beings capable of suffering, and this Constitution imposes a duty on the state, and on every person, to avoid causing them unnecessary pain, fear, or distress.

**Article 32.1.2 (Universal Minimum Floor).** Every region sets its own detailed animal-welfare rules, but no region may fall below this floor: (a) prohibition of gratuitous cruelty; (b) a duty to provide adequate food, water, shelter, and veterinary care to animals in human custody; (c) prohibition of abandonment; (d) humane methods required for any lawful killing, including for food (see 32.4).

**Article 32.1.3 (Wildlife).** Wild animals and their habitats receive protection under Part 32.5 (Protected Species) and Part 13 (Environment); this Article governs animals in human custody or affected by human activity generally.

**Article 32.1.4 (Enforcement).** Each region maintains an Animal Welfare Authority with inspection and prosecution powers; egregious or repeated cruelty is a crime under 32.2.

*Design Note: The floor is deliberately a floor, not a ceiling — consistent with the "rights are floors" principle used throughout this Constitution — so that regions with stronger cultural or religious commitments to animal protection may always go further.*

**Cross-references:** 1.4 (Animal Rights, Part 01), 32.2 (Cruelty Crimes), 32.4 (Meat and Dietary Law), 24-agriculture/animal-welfare.md


## Implementation Detail

**Article 32.1.5 (Inspection Frequency).** Commercial animal-custody operations (farms, transport, research facilities) are inspected at minimum annually, with unannounced inspections permitted at any time on credible complaint.

**Article 32.1.6 (Minimum Space and Care Standards).** Each region publishes species-specific minimum space, light, and veterinary-access standards within two years of this Constitution's adoption, reviewed every five years by independent veterinary science panels.
