# Section 13.1 — Environmental Rights

> **Part 13 — Environment and Natural Resources** · Articles 13.1.1 – 13.1.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section implements the constitutional right to a healthy environment of Section 1.12 through standards, impact assessment, participation, and access to justice.*

## Article 13.1.1 — Implementation Through Standards

1. The State shall implement the right stated in Article 1.12.1 (Right to a Healthy Environment) by maintaining binding environmental quality standards for air, water, soil, and noise.
2. Standards shall be set by law at levels protective of human health and ecosystems and shall be reviewed periodically in the light of scientific evidence.

## Article 13.1.2 — Environmental Impact Assessment

1. Projects and plans likely to have significant effects on the environment may be authorized only after an assessment of their environmental impact.
2. The assessment shall consider reasonable alternatives and mitigation measures, and its findings shall condition the decision that follows.

## Article 13.1.3 — Information and Participation

1. Every person has the right of access to environmental information held by public bodies, in accordance with Article 21.3.5 (Open Data and Reuse).
2. The public shall be given early and effective opportunities to participate in the preparation of environmental plans, programs, and significant permitting decisions, and due account shall be taken of the participation.

## Article 13.1.4 — Access to Justice

1. Any person whose rights under Section 1.12 are affected, and associations acting in the public interest under conditions defined by law, shall have standing to challenge acts or omissions contrary to environmental law.
2. Environmental proceedings shall be expeditious, and interim measures may be ordered where serious or irreversible harm is alleged.
3. The costs of environmental proceedings shall not be prohibitive.

## Article 13.1.5 — Monitoring and Enforcement

1. The State shall operate a public environmental monitoring network and publish its data.
2. Public authorities shall enforce environmental law against all persons equally, and enforcement failures shall be actionable under Article 1.12.3 (Standing and Access to Justice).

## Article 13.1.6 — Guiding Principles

1. Prevention, precaution, correction at source, and the polluter-pays principle shall guide the formulation and application of all environmental measures under this Part.

## Design Note

*Rather than restating the right, this section builds the machinery that makes Section 1.12 enforceable: standards with teeth, assessment before authorization, open information, and broad standing. Wide standing is the load-bearing choice, since environmental harm is diffuse and individual injury is often hard to prove; the cost rule prevents standing from becoming nominal. The procedural triad of information, participation, and justice parallels the structure of the Aarhus Convention of 1998.*

**Cross-references:** Article 1.12.1 (Right to a Healthy Environment); Article 1.12.3 (Standing and Access to Justice); Article 21.3.5 (Open Data and Reuse).
