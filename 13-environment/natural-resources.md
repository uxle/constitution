# Section 13.8 — Natural Resources

> **Part 13 — Environment and Natural Resources** · Articles 13.8.1 – 13.8.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section vests natural resources in the Republic for the benefit of all, including future generations, and channels extraction revenue into a sovereign wealth fund.*

## Article 13.8.1 — Public Ownership

1. Natural resources within the territory of the Republic belong to the Republic.
2. The State shall exercise resource ownership for the benefit of all, including future generations, and shall manage resources on a sustainable basis.

## Article 13.8.2 — Transparent Extraction Licensing

1. Extraction of natural resources may be authorized only under a license granted through open, competitive, and non-discriminatory procedures established by law.
2. The criteria for granting licenses, the identities of licensees, and the material terms of licenses shall be published.

## Article 13.8.3 — Sovereign Wealth Fund

1. Revenue from the extraction of non-renewable natural resources shall be paid into a sovereign wealth fund established by law.
2. The law shall govern the fund's saving and withdrawal rules, with the aim of preserving the value of resource endowments for future generations and stabilizing public finance against commodity cycles.
3. The fund shall be managed prudently and independently of day-to-day political direction, and its accounts shall be audited under Article 8.8.2 (Mandate).

## Article 13.8.4 — Transparency of Revenues

1. All payments made by resource companies to the State, and all revenues received, shall be published.
2. The beneficial ownership of licensees shall be recorded in a public register.

## Article 13.8.5 — Environmental and Social Safeguards

1. Extraction activities are subject to the impact-assessment and participation requirements of Article 13.1.2 (Environmental Impact Assessment).
2. License conditions shall include financial guarantees for site reclamation, and consultation obligations toward affected communities shall apply consistently with Article 28.5.3 (Consultation and Consent).

## Article 13.8.6 — Sharing of Benefits

1. The law may assign a defined share of resource revenues to Provinces and Municipalities affected by extraction, without diminishing the national character of resource ownership.

## Design Note

*Resource ownership is vested in the Republic as a whole to prevent local capture, yet benefit-sharing and consultation clauses acknowledge that extraction costs fall on specific places. The sovereign wealth fund converts an exhaustible endowment into a permanent financial asset and insulates the budget from commodity cycles; its intergenerational purpose is the fiscal expression of the sustainability principle in Article 8.7.1 (Principle of Sustainability). Full publication of payments and beneficial ownership follows the international anti-corruption consensus for extractive industries.*

**Cross-references:** Article 8.7.1 (Principle of Sustainability); Article 8.8.2 (Mandate); Article 13.1.2 (Environmental Impact Assessment).
