# Section 22.1 — Social Security

> **Part 22 — Social Policy** · Articles 22.1.1 – 22.1.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section establishes the right to social protection and the universal, adequate, and portable system that implements it.*

## Article 22.1.1 — Right to Social Protection

1. Every person within the Republic has the right to social protection against the standard risks of life, including sickness, unemployment, old age, disability, loss of a breadwinner, work injury, and the costs of raising children.
2. Social protection shall combine contributory insurance with non-contributory assistance so that no person is left without a minimum level of protection.
3. The Parliament shall by law define the benefits, contributions, and eligibility conditions of the system.

## Article 22.1.2 — Universal Coverage

1. Coverage shall extend to every worker irrespective of the form of employment, including part-time, temporary, self-employed, and platform work, and to every resident irrespective of employment status.
2. Periods of caregiving for children or for dependent relatives shall count as periods of insurance.
3. Gaps in coverage arising from interrupted careers shall be remedied by law and not left to administrative discretion.

## Article 22.1.3 — Adequacy and Indexation

1. Benefits shall be sufficient to secure a life in dignity, including adequate food, clothing, and housing.
2. Benefit levels shall be indexed to the cost of living and reviewed at least every three years against wage developments.
3. Minimum benefit levels shall be set by Act after consultation with representative organizations of beneficiaries and with the social partners.

## Article 22.1.4 — Fair Administration

1. Benefits shall be administered promptly, transparently, and accessibly; applications may be made in person and through digital channels of equal standing.
2. Decisions shall be reasoned in writing and shall disclose the data on which they rest; automated decisions shall remain subject to human review.
3. Every person may appeal an adverse decision to an independent body, and legal assistance shall be available as provided by law.

## Article 22.1.5 — Equality and Portability

1. Access to social protection shall be guaranteed without discrimination.
2. Entitlements shall be portable throughout the Republic, irrespective of movement between Provinces, Municipalities, schemes, or jobs.
3. Data collected for the administration of social protection may be used only for purposes compatible with the provision of benefits.

## Design Note

*The insurance-plus-assistance architecture follows the statutory social insurance tradition of postwar Europe, while the universal floor reflects the social protection floors approach developed under the International Labour Organization. Coverage is deliberately decoupled from a lifelong standard employment biography, because working lives are now intermittent. The trade-off is cost: universality is more expensive than narrow means-testing, but it avoids the exclusion errors and stigma of purely targeted systems. Portability keeps the promise of a unitary state that social rights do not depend on where a person lives.*
