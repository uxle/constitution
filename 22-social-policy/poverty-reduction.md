# Section 22.5 — Poverty Reduction

> **Part 22 — Social Policy** · Articles 22.5.1 – 22.5.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section requires a national poverty strategy built on transparent measurement and a guaranteed minimum income floor.*

## Article 22.5.1 — National Poverty Strategy

1. The Government shall adopt a comprehensive national strategy to reduce poverty and social exclusion, and shall renew it at least every five years.
2. The strategy shall address income poverty and its multidimensional causes, including access to housing, education, health care, and employment.
3. The strategy shall set measurable targets, and the Parliament shall be informed annually of progress.

## Article 22.5.2 — Transparent Measurement

1. The Statistics Authority shall measure poverty by officially published methods, using both an absolute poverty line and a relative threshold based on median incomes.
2. Data shall be disaggregated by region, household type, age, disability, and other relevant characteristics so that hidden deprivation is visible.
3. The methods of measurement may be changed only on the basis of published reasons and shall remain comparable over time.

## Article 22.5.3 — Minimum Income Floor

1. Every person lacking sufficient resources has the right to a minimum income floor ensuring a life in dignity.
2. The floor shall be set by Act, shall be indexed to the cost of living, and shall at least cover adequate food, clothing, housing, and access to essential services.
3. Conditions attached to the floor, including activation requirements, shall be reasonable; sanctions shall not reduce support below the level needed for the essentials of life.

## Article 22.5.4 — Evaluation and Participation

1. The strategy and the level of the minimum income floor shall be evaluated periodically against the measurements under Article 22.5.2.
2. Persons affected by poverty and organizations representing them shall be heard in the preparation and evaluation of the strategy.
3. The results of evaluation shall be published and shall inform the renewal of the strategy.

## Design Note

*Guaranteeing a floor by constitution, while leaving its level to ordinary law, follows the design logic of social rights in the constitutions of Finland and Portugal: the norm is justiciable as a minimum core, but fiscal calibration remains democratic. Requiring dual absolute and relative measurement is a deliberate hedge, since each indicator alone understates or overstates deprivation. The limit on sanctions reflects the lesson of conditionality experiments that withdrawal below subsistence undermines the very employability such regimes seek to build.*
