# Section 22.3 — Disability and Inclusion

> **Part 22 — Social Policy** · Articles 22.3.1 – 22.3.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section guarantees equality, reasonable accommodation, and accessibility for persons with disabilities, and supports their full participation in society.*

## Article 22.3.1 — Equality and Inclusion

1. Persons with disabilities are equal before the law and are entitled to inclusion in all areas of life without discrimination.
2. The State shall remove barriers that hinder participation in employment, education, culture, sport, and public life.
3. Affirmative measures to advance equality of persons with disabilities are permitted and shall be reviewed for effectiveness.

## Article 22.3.2 — Reasonable Accommodation

1. Public authorities shall provide reasonable accommodation so that persons with disabilities can exercise their rights on an equal basis with others.
2. Private actors shall provide reasonable accommodation where this is capable of application under the general law, and denial of reasonable accommodation shall constitute discrimination.
3. Accommodation is not required where it would impose a disproportionate burden, and the burden of demonstrating disproportionality lies with the person refusing it.

## Article 22.3.3 — Accessibility and Universal Design

1. The Parliament shall by law set accessibility standards for buildings, public spaces, transport, information, and communication services.
2. Goods and services offered to the public, including digital services, shall be designed for universal use from the outset wherever possible.
3. Public procurement shall give preference to accessible solutions at comparable cost and quality.

## Article 22.3.4 — Legal Capacity and Support

1. Legal capacity is presumed for every person with a disability.
2. Supported decision-making shall be offered before any substitute decision-making arrangement is considered, in accordance with Article 16.8.2 (Supported Decision-Making).
3. Support services, including personal assistance and communication aids, shall be available according to need and not according to wealth.

## Article 22.3.5 — Participation and Monitoring

1. Organizations of persons with disabilities shall be consulted on legislation and policy affecting them.
2. An independent mechanism shall monitor the realization of the rights in this Section and report annually to the Parliament.

## Design Note

*The architecture mirrors the United Nations Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, shifting the paradigm from care to equality. Placing the burden of justifying a refusal of accommodation on the refuser follows the approach of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990. The link to supported decision-making keeps guardianship law and social policy aligned, so that neither part of the legal system treats disability as incapacity.*
