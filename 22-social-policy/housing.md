# Section 22.4 — Housing

> **Part 22 — Social Policy** · Articles 22.4.1 – 22.4.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section commits the State to adequate housing through progressive realization and protects every person from arbitrary eviction.*

## Article 22.4.1 — Adequate Housing

1. Every person has the right to adequate housing.
2. Adequate housing includes security of tenure, habitability, affordability, accessibility, proximity to work and services, and respect for cultural expressions of dwelling.
3. The State shall pursue a national housing policy directed to the progressive realization of this right by all appropriate means, including legislation, planning, and expenditure to the maximum of available resources.

## Article 22.4.2 — Housing Plan and Targets

1. The Government shall adopt a national housing plan with time-bound targets, and shall report on progress to the Parliament annually.
2. The plan shall address the needs of homeless persons, persons with disabilities, large families, and persons unable to compete in the market without support.
3. Municipalities shall integrate housing needs into their land-use plans under Section 23.5.

## Article 22.4.3 — Protection from Arbitrary Eviction

1. No person shall be evicted from a home except on the basis of law, by a competent authority, and in accordance with due process.
2. Eviction measures shall be proportionate; before an eviction is carried out, the authorities shall consult the affected persons and examine alternatives, including the rehousing of households in vulnerable situations.
3. Evictions shall not be carried out in a manner that leaves persons without shelter in conditions incompatible with human dignity.

## Article 22.4.4 — Social Housing

1. The State and the Municipalities shall maintain a social housing stock for households whose needs cannot be met in the market.
2. Allocation of social housing shall follow published criteria, shall be administered without discrimination, and shall promote mixed communities rather than segregation.

## Article 22.4.5 — Homelessness

1. The State shall maintain a coordinated strategy to prevent and reduce homelessness, combining prevention, outreach, shelter, and pathways into permanent housing.

## Design Note

*The elements of adequacy in Article 22.4.1 paraphrase the settled interpretation of the right to adequate housing under the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, notably General Comment No. 4 of the United Nations Committee on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights. Progressive realization imposes an immediate duty to move deliberately rather than a license for inaction. The eviction safeguards draw on the South African Constitutional Court's jurisprudence on meaningful engagement before evictions from informal settlements.*
