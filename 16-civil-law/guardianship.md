# Section 16.8 — Guardianship

> **Part 16 — Civil Law** · Articles 16.8.1 – 16.8.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section governs care and representation for those who need it: the presumption of capacity, supported decision-making before any substitute decision, narrowly confined guardianship, independent review, and guardianship of children without parental care.*

## Article 16.8.1 — Presumption of Capacity

1. Every adult shall be presumed to have capacity to decide and to manage the person's own affairs.
2. A finding of incapacity shall be personal, specific to the matter concerned, and based on individual assessment; generalized findings are prohibited.

## Article 16.8.2 — Supported Decision-Making

1. Before any substitute measure, the law shall provide for support that enables the person to decide, including the assistance of a trusted person, accessible formats, and advance directives.
2. A supported decision is the decision of the person; the supporter shall not decide for the person.

## Article 16.8.3 — Substitute Measures

1. Guardianship and other substitute measures may be ordered only where supported decision-making is inadequate, only for the specific matters necessary, and only for the shortest adequate time.
2. The guardian shall act in the person's best interests, shall respect the person's will and preferences so far as they can be ascertained, and shall account regularly to the supervising authority.

## Article 16.8.4 — Independent Review

1. Substitute measures shall be ordered by a court after the person has been heard with assistance, and shall be reviewed by the court at intervals fixed by law.
2. The person may appeal and may at any time apply for termination where capacity has returned or where support suffices.

## Article 16.8.5 — Guardianship of Children

1. Where parents are unable or unwilling to fulfil parental responsibility, a guardian shall be appointed for the child under judicial supervision, taking account of the child's views consistent with Article 1.15.3 (Participation of Children).
2. The guardian shall be subject to the review duties of Article 16.8.4 (Independent Review), and alternative care shall follow the standards of Section 1.15 (Rights of Children).

## Design Note

*The Section translates Article 12 of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities into a decision sequence: presumption, support, and only then substitution, with substitute measures sliced to the specific matter and the shortest adequate time. For adults, the will-and-preferences standard borrows the Convention's own language rather than the best-interests test of childhood, precisely because adults are not children. Fixed review intervals guard against guardianship drifting from a protection into a confiscation of legal personality, and the mental-health regime of Article 12.7.2 (Consent and Support) applies the same philosophy in the clinical setting.*
