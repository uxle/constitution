# Section 1.13 — Digital Rights

> **Part 01 — Rights and Freedoms** · Articles 1.13.1 – 1.13.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section extends constitutional guarantees to the digital sphere: access to the internet, online expression and privacy, and protection from unlawful surveillance.*

## Article 1.13.1 — Access to the Internet

1. Every person has the right of access to the internet on non-discriminatory terms.
2. The State shall promote universal coverage, affordability, and digital literacy.
3. A disconnection or degradation of access requires a legal basis and is subject to Article 1.1.4 (Limitation of Rights); its statutory implementation is stated in Part 17 (Digital Law).

## Article 1.13.2 — Online Expression

1. Freedom of expression applies in full to communication through digital networks; a restriction applicable offline applies online only where its purpose and effects are comparable.
2. No general obligation to monitor content may be imposed on intermediaries; specific removals or blockings require a legal basis, are subject to review, and respect the safeguards of Article 1.3.4 (Prohibition of Prior Restraint).

## Article 1.13.3 — Digital Privacy

1. The privacy guarantees of Article 1.5.1 (Right to Privacy) apply in full to digital environments, including devices, accounts, and the data they contain.
2. Access to a device or account requires the same safeguards as access to a home.

## Article 1.13.4 — Protection from Unlawful Surveillance

1. Surveillance, interception, and the collection of communications data are permissible only under a judicial warrant, for a defined purpose and period, and only where necessary under Article 1.1.4 (Limitation of Rights).
2. Indiscriminate or bulk surveillance is prohibited unless authorized by law, necessary to address a grave threat to the life of the nation, and subject to independent review.
3. A person subjected to unlawful surveillance has the right to be informed and to an effective remedy once notification no longer endangers a lawful purpose.

## Design Note

*Digital rights are drafted as extensions of offline guarantees with equal force, which closes the loophole by which conduct restricted offline migrates online to escape control. Internet access is treated as a gateway right: without it, expression, education, and participation lose much of their value in a digital society. The surveillance clause channels even exceptional bulk powers through legality, necessity, and independent review, drawing on the comparative practice of oversight bodies for intelligence activities.*
