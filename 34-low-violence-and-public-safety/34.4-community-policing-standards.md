# Section 34.4 — Community Policing Standards

## Articles

**Article 34.4.1 (De-Escalation First).** Policing standards require de-escalation training and require it as the default response, with force used only as a last resort and always proportionate to the threat.

**Article 34.4.2 (Community Oversight).** Every policing jurisdiction maintains a civilian oversight board with power to review use-of-force incidents and recommend discipline, independent of police command.

**Article 34.4.3 (Transparency).** Use-of-force incidents are logged and reported publicly in aggregate, consistent with the transparency principles of Part 27.

*Design Note: A "low violence" society requires policing that itself models low violence; this section applies the same restraint the Constitution asks of citizens to the institutions asking citizens to comply.*

**Cross-references:** 34.1, 39-security-and-international-extended/policing-standards.md, 27-transparency-and-accountability


## Implementation Detail

**Article 34.4.4 (Reporting Interval).** Use-of-force aggregate data (34.4.3) is published quarterly, with any incident resulting in serious injury or death reported to the civilian oversight board within 72 hours.
