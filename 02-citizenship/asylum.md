# Section 2.4 — Asylum

> **Part 02 — Citizenship and Migration** · Articles 2.4.1 – 2.4.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section guarantees the right to seek and enjoy asylum, the absolute prohibition of refoulement, a fair status-determination procedure with legal aid, and dignified reception.*

## Article 2.4.1 — Right to Seek and Enjoy Asylum

1. Every person has the right to seek and to enjoy asylum in the Republic from persecution.
2. No person shall be penalized for entering or remaining in the territory irregularly where the person claims protection without delay or shows good cause for the delay.

## Article 2.4.2 — Non-Refoulement

1. No person shall be removed, returned, extradited, or expelled to a territory where the person faces a real risk of persecution, of torture, or of cruel, inhuman, or degrading treatment or punishment.
2. This prohibition is absolute; it admits no exception, no balancing, and no limitation.

## Article 2.4.3 — Fair Status Determination

1. Every protection claim shall be determined individually, in a procedure that is fair and prompt, by a competent authority with decisions subject to independent appeal.
2. The applicant shall be heard in person, with an interpreter where needed, and shall have access to the evidence relied upon.
3. An adverse decision may be appealed with suspensive effect where the appeal invokes Article 2.4.2 (Non-Refoulement).

## Article 2.4.4 — Legal Aid and Information

1. An applicant who lacks sufficient means is entitled to state-funded legal aid in the procedure, consistent with the legal-aid regime of Part 05 (The Judiciary).
2. Applicants shall receive information, in a language they understand, on the procedure, on their rights, and on the assistance available.

## Article 2.4.5 — Dignified Reception

1. Applicants shall receive reception conditions ensuring a dignified standard of living, including shelter, food, basic healthcare, and schooling for children.
2. Detention of an applicant is permissible only exceptionally, under judicial authorization, and never as a deterrent.

## Design Note

*Non-refoulement is drafted as absolute, without balancing or exception, because returning a person to persecution or torture is a harm the Constitution cannot remedy after the fact; the formulation follows the international prohibition of refoulement and the absolute ban on torture. Suspensive appeal rights operationalize the prohibition, since an unappealable removal would render the guarantee illusory. Reception standards and the exceptional status of detention keep the process humane without converting it into an incentive structure.*
