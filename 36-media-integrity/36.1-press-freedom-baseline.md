# Section 36.1 — Press Freedom Baseline

## Articles

**Article 36.1.1 (Guarantee).** Freedom of the press is guaranteed and may not be restricted by prior censorship; restrictions after publication must meet the strict proportionality test of Article 1.1.4.

**Article 36.1.2 (Source Protection).** Journalists may not be compelled to reveal confidential sources except by court order in the narrowest, most serious circumstances (e.g., preventing imminent loss of life), consistent with 27-transparency-and-accountability/whistleblower-protection.md.

**Article 36.1.3 (No State Monopoly).** The State may fund public broadcasting (21-media-and-information/public-broadcasting.md) but may never hold a monopoly over news media or criminalize criticism of the government as such.

*Design Note: This baseline is placed here, alongside the anti-corruption Articles of 36.2–36.4, deliberately: a free press and an incorruptible press are two halves of the same guarantee against unaccountable power.*

**Cross-references:** 1.2 (Freedom of Expression), 21-media-and-information, 36.2


## Implementation Detail

**Article 36.1.4 (Source Protection Order Threshold).** A court order compelling source disclosure under Article 36.1.2 requires a documented, imminent, and otherwise unpreventable risk to life, reviewable on an expedited appeal within 14 days.
