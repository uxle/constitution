# Section 36.2 — Media Ownership Transparency

## Articles

**Article 36.2.1 (Public Beneficial-Ownership Register).** All media outlets above a defined audience threshold must disclose their true beneficial owners in a public register, updated within 30 days of any change of control.

**Article 36.2.2 (Anti-Concentration Rule).** No single owner or affiliated group may control more than a defined share of any regional media market, to prevent both government and private capture of the public information space.

**Article 36.2.3 (Political Ownership Conflict).** A sitting officeholder or their immediate family may not hold a controlling stake in a media outlet operating in their own jurisdiction, to prevent officeholders from owning the outlets meant to hold them accountable.

*Design Note: Media corruption most often begins with hidden ownership, not with individual bad reporting — transparency of ownership is therefore the highest-leverage single rule in this Part.*

## Implementation Detail

**Article 36.2.4 (Concentration Cap).** No single owner or affiliated group may control more than 30% of any regional news-media market by audience share, reviewed annually by the Integrity Branch.
