# Section 42.11 — Circular Economy

## Articles

**Article 42.11.1 (Design for Reuse).** Manufacturers of durable goods must meet minimum repairability and recyclability standards, enforced jointly with the producer-responsibility rules of Part 35.2.

**Article 42.11.2 (Right to Repair).** Consumers and independent repair providers have a legal right to repair manuals, spare parts, and diagnostic tools at fair cost, overriding contrary manufacturer restrictions.

*Design Note: This is the economic engine behind the Clean Cities standard in Part 35 — a truly clean, low-waste society requires goods designed to last and be repaired, not only better bins.*
