# Section 42.12 — Civil Society

## Articles

**Article 42.12.1 (Freedom of Association Reaffirmed).** Non-governmental organizations, unions, and civic associations may form, operate, and receive funding (domestic or foreign, subject to the transparency disclosure of Part 27) without government pre-approval of their positions or activities.

**Article 42.12.2 (Protection from Reprisal).** Civil society actors are protected from state reprisal — deregistration, tax harassment, or surveillance — for lawful criticism of government, enforceable before the Constitutional Court.

*Design Note: Concentrating power is easier when independent civic organizations are weak; this section protects the "third sector" as a structural counterweight, alongside the free press of Part 36.*
