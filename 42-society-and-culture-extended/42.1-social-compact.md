# Section 42.1 — The Social Compact

## Articles

**Article 42.1.1 (Statement of Compact).** The State and its people owe each other reciprocal duties: the State guarantees the rights and services set out across this Constitution; the citizen owes civic participation, honest taxation, lawful conduct, and stewardship of shared spaces and resources (see Part 35, Part 32).

**Article 42.1.2 (Solidarity Principle).** Public policy must weigh the impact on the most vulnerable — children, the elderly, the disabled, the poor — as a matter of constitutional duty, not discretionary generosity.

*Design Note: This section exists to state, in one place, the reciprocal logic that runs through every other Part — rights and duties as a two-way compact, not a one-way grant from ruler to subject.*
