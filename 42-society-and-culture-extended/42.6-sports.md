# Section 42.6 — Sport and Physical Culture

## Articles

**Article 42.6.1 (Right to Participate).** Every person has a right of access to public sporting and recreational facilities, regardless of income, consistent with the education and health duties in Parts 11 and 12.

**Article 42.6.2 (Anti-Doping and Integrity).** National and world sporting bodies are bound by anti-doping and match-fixing integrity standards, enforced through the Integrity Branch's sporting-integrity unit (Part 37).

**Article 42.6.3 (Youth Sport Safety).** Youth sport programs must meet child-safeguarding standards under Part 38 (Juvenile Justice) and Part 01 (Children's Rights).

*Design Note: Sport is included not as decoration but because physical culture is a genuine, low-cost lever for the low-violence and public-health goals of Parts 34 and 12.*
