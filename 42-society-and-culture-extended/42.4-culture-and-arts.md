# Section 42.4 — Culture and the Arts

## Articles

**Article 42.4.1 (Cultural Rights Affirmed).** Every community's language, art, music, and cultural practice is protected and may not be suppressed by the State, consistent with Part 01 (Cultural Rights) and Part 43's protection of minorities and indigenous peoples.

**Article 42.4.2 (Public Funding, No Content Control).** The State may fund cultural institutions (museums, theatres, heritage sites) but may not condition that funding on ideological content, to prevent state capture of artistic expression.

**Article 42.4.3 (Heritage Preservation).** Sites and practices of significant cultural heritage are registered and protected from destruction or unauthorized commercial exploitation.

*Design Note: 42.4.2 mirrors the press-freedom logic of Part 36 — public funding must never become a leash on expression.*
