# Section 42.5 — Media Pluralism

## Articles

**Article 42.5.1 (Pluralism Duty).** Beyond the ownership-transparency rules of Part 36, the State has an affirmative duty to support a plurality of independent voices — including minority-language and regional outlets — through non-discretionary funding formulas.

**Article 42.5.2 (Public Broadcaster Independence).** The public broadcaster (21-media-and-information/public-broadcasting.md) is governed by an independent board insulated from the government of the day, funded by a formula set in statute rather than annual political negotiation.

*Design Note: Ownership transparency (Part 36) stops bad actors from capturing media; pluralism funding (this section) proactively ensures enough independent actors exist in the first place.*
