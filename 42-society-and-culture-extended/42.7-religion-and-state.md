# Section 42.7 — Religion and the State

## Articles

**Article 42.7.1 (No State Religion, Full Freedom).** The State establishes no official religion and treats all faiths, and the absence of faith, with equal standing before the law, consistent with Article 1.4 (Freedom of Religion and Conscience).

**Article 42.7.2 (Accommodation of Religious Custom).** Regional and local law may accommodate religious custom — holidays, dietary practice (Part 32.4), dress, and family law variations — provided it does not fall below the universal rights floor of Part 01.

**Article 42.7.3 (No Compulsion).** No person may be compelled to participate in a religious observance, nor penalized in employment, education, or public service for their religion or lack of it.

*Design Note: This is the same subsidiarity logic used for meat consumption in 32.4 — one rights floor everywhere, local custom governing everything above it, so a world constitution does not have to flatten every culture's religious life to be workable.*
