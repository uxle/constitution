# Section 42.10 — Oceans and Marine Governance

## Articles

**Article 42.10.1 (Marine Protected Areas).** A world network of Marine Protected Areas is established and expanded on independent scientific review, paralleling the terrestrial protections of Part 32.3 and Part 13.

**Article 42.10.2 (Sustainable Fisheries).** Fishing quotas are set by independent scientific assessment of stock health, not by short-term economic pressure, coordinated with 24-agriculture/fisheries.md.

**Article 42.10.3 (Marine Pollution).** Dumping of plastic, chemical, or industrial waste into any waterway or ocean is a crime under 38-penal-code-extended/environmental-crime.md.

*Design Note: Oceans cross every regional boundary the rest of this Constitution otherwise respects, which is why they are governed at the world level rather than left to regional customary law, unlike diet (32.4) or religion (42.7).*
