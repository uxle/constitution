# Section 42.8 — Integration Policy

## Articles

**Article 42.8.1 (Two-Way Integration).** Integration of migrants and newcomers is treated as a two-way process: the State provides language and civic-orientation programs, and receiving communities are supported in accommodating newcomers, rather than integration being framed as an obligation on migrants alone.

**Article 42.8.2 (Non-Discrimination in Access).** Public services (education, health, employment support) are available to lawful residents regardless of length of residence, subject to the citizenship distinctions set out in Part 02.

*Design Note: Framing integration as reciprocal (42.8.1) is deliberate — one-way integration mandates have a documented record of breeding resentment rather than cohesion.*
