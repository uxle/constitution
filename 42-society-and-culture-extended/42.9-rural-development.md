# Section 42.9 — Rural Development

## Articles

**Article 42.9.1 (Rural Infrastructure Parity).** Rural and highland communities (see Part 33) are entitled to the same infrastructure investment ratio, per capita, as urban communities, reviewed by the Auditor-General (20-independent-institutions/auditor-general.md).

**Article 42.9.2 (Agricultural Livelihood Protection).** Land-use and trade policy (Part 09, Part 24) must account for the livelihood impact on smallholder and rural communities before implementation.

*Design Note: This section is the rural counterpart to the highland-specific protections in Part 33, extending the same "no permanent neglect" principle to all rural, not only mountainous, communities.*
