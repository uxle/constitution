# Section 42.13 — Youth Policy

## Articles

**Article 42.13.1 (Youth Voice).** Regions must maintain a youth advisory body with a formal channel to submit views into the legislative process, particularly on education, environment, and long-term fiscal policy that will bear most heavily on their generation.

**Article 42.13.2 (Transition to Adulthood Support).** Young people leaving state care, education, or the justice system are guaranteed a transition-support program (housing, employment guidance) consistent with 38-penal-code-extended/parole-and-reentry.md and Part 22.

*Design Note: This section connects to the Future Generations Council of Part 40, giving young people both a long-term institutional voice (there) and a direct, present-day legislative channel (here).*
