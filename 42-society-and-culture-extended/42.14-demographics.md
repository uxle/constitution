# Section 42.14 — Demographics

## Articles

**Article 42.14.1 (Demographic Monitoring).** The State maintains independent demographic monitoring (aging, birth rates, migration flows) through its Statistics Authority (20-independent-institutions/statistics-authority.md), published transparently to inform, not dictate, policy.

**Article 42.14.2 (No Coercive Population Policy).** The State may never adopt coercive measures — forced sterilization, birth quotas, or punitive childlessness taxes — to influence population size; family-size decisions remain a private matter under Article 1.5 (Privacy) and Article 16 (Family Law).

*Design Note: 42.14.2 is written as an explicit prohibition, not a silence, because demographic anxiety has historically been the justification for some of the worst rights violations in real constitutional history; naming the prohibition directly closes that door.*
