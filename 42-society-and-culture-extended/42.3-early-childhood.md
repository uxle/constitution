# Section 42.3 — Early Childhood

## Articles

**Article 42.3.1 (Universal Early-Childhood Access).** Every child has guaranteed access to early-childhood care and pre-primary education from age three, funded as part of the compulsory education system (Part 11).

**Article 42.3.2 (Nutrition and Health Screening).** Early-childhood programs include mandatory nutrition and basic health screening, linked to the healthcare-funding provisions of Part 41.

*Design Note: Investment here is treated as the highest-return point in the whole education system, consistent with the evidence base most existing school systems already act on.*
