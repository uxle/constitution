# Section 42.2 — Family Policy

## Articles

**Article 42.2.1 (Family Support).** The State supports families through parental leave, childcare access, and family counselling services, without prescribing any single family structure as the only legitimate one.

**Article 42.2.2 (Non-Discrimination Among Family Forms).** Married couples, unmarried partners, single parents, and extended-family households receive equal legal recognition and support, consistent with Article 1.2 (Equality).

*Design Note: Family policy is placed here, separate from the Bill of Rights, because it is about affirmative support programs, not the underlying right — the right to found a family is already protected in Part 01/16.*
