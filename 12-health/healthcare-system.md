# Section 12.1 — Healthcare System

> **Part 12 — Health** · Articles 12.1.1 – 12.1.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section organizes the health system so that the right of access to healthcare under Section 1.11 is realized through universal coverage, strong primary care, and enforceable guarantees of timely treatment.*

## Article 12.1.1 — Universal Access

1. The State shall ensure that every person within the Republic can obtain necessary healthcare services without financial hardship, in fulfillment of the right stated in Article 1.11.1 (Right of Access to Healthcare Services).
2. Parliament shall establish by law a coverage scheme that:
   (a) defines the health services to which access is guaranteed;
   (b) is financed from public revenues and, as the law provides, compulsory contributions;
   (c) is portable across the whole territory of the Republic.

## Article 12.1.2 — Public Financing Floor

1. Public financing of the coverage scheme shall be sufficient to secure its minimum core obligations and shall not fall below the level needed to maintain existing services.
2. Cost-sharing may be required only as the law provides and shall not operate as a barrier for persons without means.
3. No person shall be denied emergency care for inability to pay, consistent with Article 1.11.2 (Emergency Care).

## Article 12.1.3 — Primary and Community Care

1. The health system shall be organized around accessible primary and community care, supported by referral to secondary and tertiary services.
2. The State shall secure rural and remote coverage through fixed facilities, outreach, and mobile and telemedicine services.
3. Regions may coordinate health planning across Provinces under Article 7.2.2 (Competences).

## Article 12.1.4 — Waiting-Time Guarantees

1. Parliament shall set by law maximum waiting times for defined categories of care.
2. An independent authority shall monitor compliance with those guarantees and publish its findings.
3. Where a guaranteed waiting time is exceeded, the patient shall have the right to obtain the treatment elsewhere at the cost of the coverage scheme.

## Article 12.1.5 — Choice of Provider

1. Patients may choose among providers participating in the coverage scheme.
2. Private provision of healthcare is lawful, subject to licensing and to the standards applicable to public providers.
3. The terms on which private providers participate in the coverage scheme shall be public and non-discriminatory.

## Article 12.1.6 — Workforce and Equal Access

1. The State shall plan for a sufficient health workforce and shall promote an equitable distribution of professionals across regions and specialties, with incentives for service in underserved areas.
2. Health facilities and services shall be physically and informationally accessible to persons with disabilities, consistent with Article 28.3.3 (Accessibility).

## Design Note

*This section turns the right of access under Section 1.11 into an administrable system: a statutory benefit package, a financing floor, and a waiting-time guarantee with a concrete remedy. The trade-off is that a benefit package defined by law forces prioritization decisions into Parliament rather than into clinical discretion or the courts. The waiting-time remedy borrows from targeted-treatment guarantees in European coverage systems.*
