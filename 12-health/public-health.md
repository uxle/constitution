# Section 12.2 — Public Health

> **Part 12 — Health** · Articles 12.2.1 – 12.2.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section governs preventive care, health promotion, and communicable-disease powers, subjecting every coercive measure to legality, proportionality, and independent scientific advice.*

## Article 12.2.1 — Preventive Care

1. The State shall provide preventive services, including immunization programs, screening for treatable conditions, and health education, as part of the coverage scheme under Article 12.1.1 (Universal Access).
2. Participation in immunization programs may be encouraged by proportionate incentives and, for defined diseases, required by law subject to Article 12.2.4 (Scientific Advice and Transparency).

## Article 12.2.2 — Health in All Policies

1. Public authorities shall consider the effects of their decisions on population health and health equity.
2. Environmental, food-safety, and occupational-health authorities shall coordinate their standard-setting with the health authority.

## Article 12.2.3 — Communicable-Disease Surveillance

1. The health authority shall operate systems for the surveillance and notification of communicable diseases.
2. Personal data collected for surveillance shall be handled under the safeguards of Article 12.6.1 (Specially Protected Data) and used only for public-health purposes.

## Article 12.2.4 — Scientific Advice and Transparency

1. An independent scientific body shall advise the health authority on measures with major public-health impact.
2. The evidence and reasoning supporting such measures shall be published, and dissenting scientific opinions shall be recorded.

## Article 12.2.5 — Limits on Coercive Measures

1. Isolation, quarantine, mandatory examination, and treatment orders may be imposed only by law, on the basis of the best available evidence, and only where necessary and proportionate to the risk.
2. Such measures shall be the least restrictive available, shall be time-limited, and shall be subject to prompt review by a court or independent body.
3. Restrictive measures shall not discriminate and shall not be used for any purpose other than the protection of health.

## Article 12.2.6 — Emergencies and Compensation

1. The ordinary powers of this section apply unless a public-health emergency is declared under Article 26.3.1 (Graduated Response).
2. Persons bearing an extraordinary and disproportionate burden imposed for the protection of public health shall be entitled to compensation as the law provides.

## Design Note

*Communicable-disease powers are placed on a strict legality-and-proportionality footing because history shows they can be stretched far beyond epidemiological need. The independent scientific-advice requirement insulates decision-making from short-term politics while keeping the evidence public. The compensation clause borrows from quarantine compensation traditions and disciplines the State against casual recourse to coercion. Ordinary surveillance and emergency regimes are kept distinct so that temporary powers do not silently become permanent.*
