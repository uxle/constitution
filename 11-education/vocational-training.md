# Section 11.5 — Vocational Training

> **Part 11 — Education** · Articles 11.5.1 – 11.5.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section guarantees vocational education and training as a pathway of equal standing, organizes apprenticeships under tripartite governance, and ensures that credentials gained are portable and renewable throughout working life.*

## Article 11.5.1 — Vocational Pathways

1. The State shall promote vocational education and training as a pathway of equal standing with general education, open to all without discrimination.
2. Vocational programs shall confer qualifications respected by employers and by institutions of general and higher education, and movement between pathways shall remain open, consistent with Section 11.1.

## Article 11.5.2 — Apprenticeships

1. Parliament shall establish by law the conditions of apprenticeship and other work-based learning, including written agreements, remuneration, working-time limits, safety, mentoring, and the protection of health.
2. Persons below the age of eighteen may be engaged in work-based learning only within the protections applicable to working minors under Article 10.2.2 (Working Minors).

## Article 11.5.3 — Tripartite Governance

1. The Government, the representative organizations of employers, and the representative organizations of workers shall participate together in designing curricula, examining candidates, and certifying work-based training.
2. Parliament may establish levies or shared schemes so that the cost of training is not borne only by employers who train apprentices whom others later employ.

## Article 11.5.4 — Portability of Credentials

1. All vocational qualifications shall be defined within the national qualifications framework, shall certify demonstrated competencies rather than time spent in instruction, and shall be portable across the Republic, consistent with Article 11.3.3 (Recognition of Qualifications).
2. Competencies acquired informally or in prior employment shall be assessable and creditable toward a qualification.

## Article 11.5.5 — Retraining for Displaced Workers

1. The State shall provide retraining opportunities for workers whose employment is displaced by technological or economic change, with income support where necessary, in coordination with the training rights under Article 10.8.4 (Training Rights).
2. The State shall ensure impartial information and career guidance about vocational and educational pathways, available to learners and workers at every stage of life and free of charge to the individual learner.

## Design Note

*The dual apprenticeship models of Germany and Switzerland show how employer participation secures labor-market relevance, and their insight is constitutionalized here as tripartite governance rather than left to custom. The competency-based, portable credential follows ILO Recommendation No. 195 of 2004, which treats skills as assets that must survive job changes. The levy clause addresses poaching: collective schemes prevent firms that train from subsidizing firms that do not. The accepted trade-off is that social-partner co-design embeds training in current demand, which can slow adaptation to new technologies.*

**Cross-references:** Article 10.8.4 (Training Rights); Article 11.3.3 (Recognition of Qualifications); Article 10.2.2 (Working Minors).
