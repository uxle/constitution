# Section 11.6 — Academic Freedom

> **Part 11 — Education** · Articles 11.6.1 – 11.6.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section protects the freedom to inquire, to teach, and to publish; the security of the individuals who exercise that freedom; and the limits that scientific integrity and research ethics impose.*

## Article 11.6.1 — Freedom of Research

1. Research shall be free. Every researcher shall be free to choose methods and fields of inquiry, to pursue knowledge within and across disciplines, and to publish results without prior approval.
2. No public or private interest shall require research to follow predetermined conclusions, and the independence of research results shall be protected as provided in Article 1.10.4 (Academic freedom).

## Article 11.6.2 — Freedom of Teaching

1. Teaching shall be free. Every teacher shall be free to present and discuss scholarly positions within the framework of the curriculum and to express academic judgments without fear of sanction.
2. No test of political, religious, or ideological conformity shall be applied to admission, appointment, or promotion in any institution of learning.

## Article 11.6.3 — Protection of Individuals

1. No researcher, teacher, or student shall be dismissed, disciplined, or denied advancement for lawful research or expression, and the protection of expression under Article 1.3.1 (Freedom of expression) applies in full within institutions of learning.
2. Institutions shall establish fair procedures, before independent bodies, for decisions affecting the status of academic staff and students.

## Article 11.6.4 — Limits

1. Academic freedom may be limited only by law, for a legitimate aim, and to the extent strictly necessary in a democratic society.
2. Research involving human subjects, personal data, or dangerous biological material may be regulated for the protection of those interests, and nothing in this section shall authorize conduct prohibited by criminal law.

## Article 11.6.5 — Research Integrity and Ethics

1. Research shall be conducted with honesty; plagiarism, fabrication, and falsification constitute misconduct carrying consequences fixed by law or by the statutes of the institution.
2. Research involving human subjects or personal data requires prior independent ethical review, consistent with the protections of personal data under the law.

## Design Note

*The guarantee synthesizes the UNESCO Recommendation of 1997 concerning the Status of Higher-Education Teaching Personnel and the research-and-teaching clause of the German Basic Law, extending protection to students as well as teachers. The clause forbidding predetermined conclusions answers both political steering and donor pressure, the twin masters to which research is most often sold. Ethics review appears as a constitutional limit so that protection of subjects and integrity of method discipline inquiry from within rather than by administrative fiat. The accepted trade-off is that strong protection makes research slower to redirect, even when public priorities change quickly.*
