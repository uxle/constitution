# Section 11.7 — Education Finance

> **Part 11 — Education** · Articles 11.7.1 – 11.7.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section fixes the duty of the State to fund education adequately and equitably — a guaranteed minimum share of public expenditure, per-pupil funding weighted for need, transparent reporting, and the maintenance of schools.*

## Article 11.7.1 — Guaranteed Minimum Share

1. Public expenditure on education shall not fall below the minimum share of total public expenditure prescribed by law, and the law shall set that share by reference to international benchmarks.
2. The guaranteed share shall be appropriated through the annual budget under Article 8.2.3 (Parliamentary Adoption) and shall give priority to basic and compulsory education.
3. Reductions below the guaranteed share are impermissible; reductions within it shall be gradual, reasoned, and assessed for their effect on learners before they take effect.

## Article 11.7.2 — Equitable Per-Pupil Funding

1. Funds shall be distributed among institutions by a published formula weighted for need, including regional cost, learner disadvantage, and disability, so that the quality of education does not depend on the wealth of the community.
2. The weights shall be reviewed when regional costs or pupil needs change, and targeted funds shall be used only for their stated purpose.

## Article 11.7.3 — Transparent Reporting

1. Public expenditure on education shall be reported annually, with its share of public expenditure and of gross domestic product published and its distribution among levels of education shown separately.
2. Per-learner funding shall be published so that residents can compare institutions, and the terms of any public-private partnership in education shall be published.
3. All public education funding shall be traceable in published budgets and audited accounts.

## Article 11.7.4 — Maintenance of Schools

1. The State shall keep public school buildings safe, accessible, and fit for instruction, and shall provide for their maintenance on a multi-year basis.
2. Public basic and secondary education shall remain free of tuition, in fulfillment of Article 11.2.1 (Free Compulsory Basic Education), and private contributions shall never condition admission.

## Article 11.7.5 — Review of Adequacy

1. Parliament shall review the adequacy of education funding at intervals not exceeding five years, taking account of demography, prices, and the cost of achieving the aims of education.
2. Educators in public service shall receive remuneration sufficient to attract and retain qualified professionals, paid reliably and on time.

## Design Note

*The guaranteed-share mechanism follows the Education 2030 benchmark tradition, which counsels roughly four to six percent of gross domestic product for education, while anchoring the floor to public expenditure so that it survives economic downturns. Needs-weighted per-pupil formulas draw on the school-finance litigation of several jurisdictions, where weighted allocation has become the standard instrument for equalizing inputs across rich and poor communities. Publishing per-learner funding converts equity from aspiration into a figure anyone can verify. The accepted trade-off is that constitutional funding floors and formulas constrain budgetary flexibility.*
