# Section 11.3 — Higher Education

> **Part 11 — Education** · Articles 11.3.1 – 11.3.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section governs access to higher education on the basis of merit, the financial support that keeps study within reach, the recognition of qualifications, and lifelong learning.*

## Article 11.3.1 — Access by Merit

1. Higher education shall be equally accessible to all on the basis of capacity, and no person shall be excluded on a ground of discrimination prohibited by law.
2. Admission procedures shall be published in advance, based on transparent criteria, and subject to appeal.
3. The State shall take measures, including preparatory and bridging programs, so that persons from disadvantaged backgrounds can compete for places on equal terms.

## Article 11.3.2 — Financial Support

1. The State shall maintain scholarships and need-based grants or loans on terms that allow qualified students to complete their studies without discrimination.
2. Fees, where charged by public institutions, shall be published, capped by law, and accompanied by exemptions for students without means.
3. Financial support shall be portable across institutions and regions within the Republic.

## Article 11.3.3 — Recognition of Qualifications

1. Degrees and periods of study completed at an accredited institution shall be recognized throughout the Republic without further examination.
2. Qualifications obtained abroad shall be assessed and recognized under transparent procedures, consistent with the international obligations of the Republic, and within reasonable time limits.
3. Accreditation of institutions and programs shall be exercised by the independent quality authority provided in Article 11.4.4 (Quality Assurance).

## Article 11.3.4 — Lifelong Learning

1. Institutions shall recognize prior learning and professional experience toward admission or credit.
2. Public institutions shall provide part-time, continuing, and distance education for persons in employment and at every age, and public support shall not be limited to full-time study.

## Article 11.3.5 — Student Rights

1. Students shall have access to syllabi and assessment criteria, fair examination procedures, and a right of appeal against academic decisions.
2. Students shall be represented in the governing bodies of institutions that receive public funds, consistent with Article 11.4.1 (Institutional Autonomy).

## Design Note

*Equal access on the basis of capacity quotes ICESCR Article 13(2)(c), which makes higher education a progressively realized but non-discriminatory entitlement. Transparent admission and appeal rights answer the arbitrariness that historically accompanied selective entry, while bridging programs address unequal preparation rather than lowering standards. Recognition of foreign qualifications follows the recognition conventions of the European region, which convert credential evaluation from discretion into procedure. The accepted trade-off is statutory fee caps, which limit institutional pricing freedom in exchange for predictable access.*
