# Section 23.1 — Transport

> **Part 23 — Infrastructure** · Articles 23.1.1 – 23.1.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section sets the objectives and instruments of transport policy: safety, accessibility, sustainability, integrated planning, and public service obligations.*

## Article 23.1.1 — Objectives of Transport Policy

1. Transport policy shall provide safe, accessible, and sustainable mobility for persons and goods throughout the Republic.
2. Safety shall be the first objective of transport policy; the State shall pursue continuous reduction of deaths and serious injuries on all transport networks.
3. Transport investment and regulation shall support the transition to low-emission mobility and shall be consistent with the environmental duties of Section 1.12.

## Article 23.1.2 — Integrated Planning

1. The Government shall adopt a national transport plan coordinating road, rail, waterborne, and air transport, and shall renew it periodically.
2. Provinces and Municipalities shall be involved in the preparation of plans that affect their territories, and local networks shall connect to the national network.
3. Major transport projects shall undergo environmental assessment in accordance with Article 13.1.2 (Environmental Impact Assessment).

## Article 23.1.3 — Public Service Obligations

1. The Parliament may by law impose public service obligations to secure basic transport services on routes that would not be commercially viable.
2. Obligations shall be defined in advance, awarded impartially under Article 8.5.2 (Procedures and Award Criteria), and compensated fairly.
3. Vehicles and infrastructure open to the public shall be accessible to persons with disabilities in accordance with Section 22.3.

## Article 23.1.4 — Safety Regulation and Investigation

1. Technical safety standards for vehicles, vessels, aircraft, and infrastructure shall be set by law and enforced by the competent authorities.
2. Independent bodies shall investigate transport accidents with a view to prevention, and their safety recommendations shall be considered publicly by the competent authorities.

## Design Note

*Making safety the first objective codifies the Vision Zero philosophy first adopted by the Swedish Parliament in 1997, which treats deaths on the network as a design failure rather than an accepted cost. Public service obligations are the standard instrument for reconciling network coverage with market operation, and the requirement of impartial award ties them to public procurement law. The Constitution prescribes objectives and coordination; mode-specific detail remains statutory.*

**Cross-references:** Article 13.1.2 (Environmental Impact Assessment); Article 8.5.2 (Procedures and Award Criteria).
