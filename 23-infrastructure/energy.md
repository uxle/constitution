# Section 23.2 — Energy

> **Part 23 — Infrastructure** · Articles 23.2.1 – 23.2.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section secures reliable, sustainable, and affordable energy for all, with transition planning and independent regulation of energy networks.*

## Article 23.2.1 — Energy Policy Objectives

1. Energy policy shall secure a reliable, environmentally sound, and affordable supply of energy for the Republic.
2. Access to energy is an essential service; disconnection of households in vulnerable situations shall be restricted by law and never endanger health or dignity.
3. Energy policy shall respect the environmental duty of the State under Article 1.12.2 (Duty of the State).

## Article 23.2.2 — Transition Planning

1. The Government shall adopt a national energy and climate plan defining the phased transition of energy production and consumption, and shall renew it periodically.
2. The plan shall take into account the technical security of supply and shall support affected workers and regions through retraining and regional development measures under Part 10 and Part 7.

## Article 23.2.3 — Universal Access and Consumer Protection

1. Every household shall have the right to a connection to the electricity network on reasonable, published terms.
2. Tariffs shall be transparent; vulnerable households shall benefit from targeted support rather than generalized underpricing that discourages conservation.

## Article 23.2.4 — Independent Network Regulation

1. Energy networks shall be regulated by an independent authority established by law.
2. The authority shall ensure non-discriminatory third-party access to networks, transparent tariff methodologies, and investment planning adequate to maintain reliability.
3. The independence and accountability of the authority shall meet the standards applicable to independent regulators under Section 29.3.

## Article 23.2.5 — Safety and Resilience

1. Technical safety standards for the production, transmission, storage, and distribution of energy shall be set and enforced by law.
2. Critical energy facilities shall meet the resilience requirements of Section 23.6.

## Design Note

*Energy touches every canonical tension in infrastructure law: monopoly networks, public goods, and climate obligations. The text answers with three stable commitments — universal connection, an independent network regulator, and phased transition planning — while leaving the generation mix entirely to democratic choice. The vulnerable-household rule follows the practice of several European regulators that prohibit winter disconnections, chosen here in dignity terms rather than seasonal ones.*

**Cross-references:** Article 1.12.2 (Duty of the State); Article 13.1.1 (Implementation Through Standards).
