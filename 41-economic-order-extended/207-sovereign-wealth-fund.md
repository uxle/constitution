# WC-207 · The Sovereign Wealth and Stabilisation Fund

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **207 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Saving windfalls for the many and the future

## Preamble

This document establishes the sovereign fund with iron governance.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Purpose

1.1 The Fund saves non-renewable revenues and windfalls, stabilises the budget against shocks, and invests for future generations.

1.2 The Fund is a constitutional organ under document 075's protections.

1.3 Fiscal rule: annual draw equals expected real return - around three percent - never principal.

### Article 2 — Governance

2.1 Board with independent majority, ethical mandates and published criteria; management is professional and indexed.

2.2 All holdings, returns, costs and votes publish quarterly; transparency beats secrecy by design.

2.3 No political instructions, no home-country rescues, no connected-party investments.

### Article 3 — Ethics and integrity

3.1 Exclusion frameworks for corruption, arms, tobacco and severe rights violators with active ownership.

3.2 The Integrity Commission audits the Fund with the full powers of document 077.

3.3 Managers and board are under the ethics code with cooling-off under document 099.

### Article 4 — Uses

4.1 Drawdowns beyond the rule require supermajority and a stated emergency under document 080.

4.2 Regional and generational equity reporting accompanies annual accounts.

4.3 Dissolution of the Fund is an unamendable-core protection for its principal.

## Commentary

Sovereign funds fail exactly where the corpus is strongest: opaque politics raiding patient capital, so the chapter gives the fund branch-level protection, a spending rule of expected return, and total publication of votes and holdings - the Norwegian standard with harder edges. Prohibiting connected-party investment and political rescues closes the documented abuse channels. Protecting the principal in the unamendable core is the strongest promise a constitution can make to its unborn.
