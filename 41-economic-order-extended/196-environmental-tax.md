# WC-196 · Environmental and Consumption Taxes

> **Part 41 — Economic Order Extended** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 196/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Pricing pollution, protecting essentials: VAT, carbon, sin taxes

## Preamble

This document designs consumption and environmental taxes with social compensation.

## Articles

### Article 1 — VAT

1.1 One value-added tax with a standard rate and a short reduced list: food, medicine, books, children's goods.

1.2 Small-business simplification with annual schemes; refunds within statutory periods.

1.3 Cross-border follows destination rules with trust-and-check controls.

### Article 2 — Carbon and pollution

2.1 Carbon pricing rises on a published trajectory to the social cost, covering fuels, industry and aviation with border adjustment for imports from unpriced jurisdictions.

2.2 Revenues recycle as per-capita dividends protecting low-income households.

2.3 Pollution taxes on NOx, waste, plastics and chemicals follow the damage-cost logic.

### Article 3 — Excise and sin

3.1 Excises on alcohol, tobacco, gambling and sugar-sweetened drinks are set with health objectives, minimum floors and smuggling-risk management.

3.2 Vaping products are taxed as tobacco-adjacent.

3.3 Gambling taxation licenses honest operators while contribution caps prevent state dependence.

### Article 4 — Fees and earmarking

4.1 User fees follow cost-recovery principles under document 175; no fee funds general budgets without statute.

4.2 Earmarking of environmental revenues is limited and transparent to the green transition accounts.

4.3 All rates publish with their evidence base and sunset under document 177.

## Commentary

Pricing pollution is the cheapest environmental policy ever tested, and per-capita dividend recycling solves its fairness problem in one stroke, which is why the carbon-plus-dividend architecture is adopted whole. Short reduced-VAT lists follow the evidence that long lists leak revenue to consultants while barely helping the poor. Border adjustment prevents honest domestic industry from being undercut by unpriced carbon imports.

## Notes for adopting states

- Fiscal rules require the independent fiscal council to be credible; appoint it before the first budget under the new regime.
- Tax reform moves as a package: base-broadening, pre-filling and compliance-gap publication together.
- The debt brake and the sovereign fund are intergenerational instruments; adopt them early, govern them forever.
