# WC-201 · Education Funding

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **201 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Fair funding formulas, teachers and lifelong learning accounts

## Preamble

This document designs the financing of education from early childhood to lifelong learning.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Formula funding

1.1 Schools are funded by transparent weighted-student formulas: base plus deprivation, language, disability and rural factors.

1.2 The formula and its parameters are public law; discretionary grants are minimal and logged under document 175.

1.3 Equalisation guarantees comparable resources per similar student across regions.

### Article 2 — Teachers

2.1 Teacher pay sits at graduate professional bands with recruitment incentives for hard-to-staff areas.

2.2 Professional development entitlements and career ladders without leaving the classroom.

2.3 Teacher supply planning is statutory with bursary pipelines.

### Article 3 — Higher education

3.1 Tuition-free public higher education for first degrees within normal duration; maintenance grants means-tested.

3.2 Income-contingent deferred fees for private or extended study.

3.3 Research funding follows the dual-support model with peer review under document 031.

### Article 4 — Lifelong

4.1 Individual learning accounts fund adult reskilling with portability between providers.

4.2 Employer training levies with sectoral governance.

4.3 Recognition of prior learning under document 017.

## Commentary

Education funding is where equality is manufactured or forfeited, and weighted formulas in public law remove the discretionary grants that fed both favouritism and neglect. Tuition-free first degrees with means-tested maintenance follow the successful European models that kept social mobility alive. Learning accounts make the system adult-friendly, which the automation transition documented as a necessity.
