# WC-194 · Personal Income Tax

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **194 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** One tax, one base, fair progressivity

## Preamble

This document designs the personal income tax as the system's progressive core.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Base

1.1 Global income taxation: employment, business, capital, pensions above thresholds, and rental income in one base.

1.2 Capital gains above a lifetime allowance join the base; the family home is exempt within limits.

1.3 Losses offset across sources with anti-avoidance rules for artificial losses.

### Article 2 — Rate structure

2.1 A progressive schedule with a top marginal rate around half, refundable credits at the bottom, and no cliff effects.

2.2 Indexation of brackets to prices; periodic real-bracket review.

2.3 Joint filing options with splitting benefits capped to avoid marriage distortions.

### Article 3 — Withholding and filing

3.1 Employers, banks and platforms withhold at source; pre-filled returns confirm in minutes under document 173.

3.2 Self-employed follow cash-basis accounting below thresholds with flat-rate options.

3.3 Filing is annual, digital, assisted and free.

### Article 4 — Credits

4.1 Refundable credits: child credit, care credit, earned-income credit, education credit.

4.2 Credits pay out monthly on request - poverty does not wait for April.

4.3 No credits for investment schemes without economic substance.

## Commentary

The income tax design follows the Nordic-global-income model with the compliance ease of the pre-filled systems, because progressivity that citizens can actually file is progressivity that survives politics. Monthly refundable credits convert the tax system into the social policy delivery layer, which is cheaper and more universal than parallel agencies. The lifetime capital-gains allowance protects honest savers while closing the realiser's escape.
