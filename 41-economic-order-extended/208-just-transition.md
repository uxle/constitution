# WC-208 · Just Transition and Industrial Policy

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **208 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Structural change with workers and regions carried, not sacrificed

## Preamble

This document governs industrial and transition policy with integrity conditions.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Mandate

1.1 Structural transitions - decarbonisation, digitalisation, deindustrialisation - are managed with statutory just-transition plans.

1.2 Plans cover employment, retraining, regional investment and social protection with funded budgets.

1.3 The Future Generations Council reviews plans under document 270.

### Article 2 — Industrial policy discipline

2.1 Horizontal instruments first: R&D, skills, infrastructure, energy; selective aid only for proven market failures.

2.2 Aid is transparent, performance-based, capped and sunset under document 177; the subsidy register publishes all beneficiaries.

2.3 Failed aid is recovered by clawback conditions.

### Article 3 — Regional

3.1 Transition regions receive priority investment funds with local governance and published milestones.

3.2 EU-style cohesion logic: convergence targets with monitoring by the fiscal council.

3.3 One-company towns get diversification plans before closure decisions.

### Article 4 — Workers

4.1 Guaranteed reemployment or income bridge for displaced workers over fifty; pension bridging where reemployment fails.

4.2 Early-warning duty on employers planning structural change.

4.3 Works councils participate in transition design under document 149.

## Commentary

Industrial policy is returning everywhere, and the corpus's condition is integrity: registers, performance, sunsets, clawbacks - otherwise subsidy becomes the polite name for patronage. Just-transition plans answer the moral charge that climate and digital transitions sacrifice the few for the many. Early-warning and works-council participation convert closures from ambushes into managed change.

- Tax reform moves as a package: base-broadening, pre-filling and compliance-gap publication together.
- The debt brake and the sovereign fund are intergenerational instruments; adopt them early, govern them forever.
