# WC-189 · Money and Currency

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **189 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Legal tender, cash rights and the payments commons

## Preamble

This document governs money: issue, legal tender, cash access and payments.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Issue and tender

1.1 The monetary unit is defined by statute; the central bank has the sole right of issue under document 078.

1.2 Banknotes and coins are legal tender for all debts public and private without limit for cash.

1.3 Digital central-bank currency, if issued, is a public payment option, never a substitute for cash or an instrument of surveillance.

### Article 2 — Cash rights

2.1 Cash acceptance is mandatory at physical points of sale below a statutory cap, with narrow exemptions filed and published.

2.2 Cash access: withdrawal and deposit points within reasonable distance maintained by geographic service duty on banks.

2.3 No person is compelled to hold a bank account to receive public payments - cash payout options remain.

### Article 3 — Payments

3.1 Payment systems are regulated infrastructure with cost-based interchange, instant payments and open access for providers.

3.2 Fees are transparent; surcharging is banned for standard instruments.

3.3 Operational resilience and outage compensation are licence conditions.

### Article 4 — Integrity of money

4.1 Counterfeiting is a Class II offence; export controls cover currency equipment.

4.2 Cash transport and processing are licensed, audited activities.

4.3 Large-cash payment limits apply above statutory thresholds with reporting, not prohibition, for private transactions.

## Commentary

Cash is the anonymous payment technology of the poor and the free, and its quiet abolition would create a surveillance chokepoint the corpus refuses; hence the cash-rights clauses with geographic access duties. Instant payments with cost-based interchange follow the strongest modern reforms, cutting the private tax on commerce. CBDC language keeps the technology public without ever making it the eye of the state.
