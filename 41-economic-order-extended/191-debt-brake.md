# WC-191 · The Public Debt Brake

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **191 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Structural balance by rule with escape clauses and transparency

## Preamble

This document implements the constitutional debt brake over all levels of government.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The rule

1.1 Structural net borrowing of the state may not exceed zero point three five percent of GDP in ordinary years; regions and municipalities balance current budgets annually.

1.2 Cyclical effects are smoothed by the economic-cycle adjustment; one-off effects are excluded transparently.

1.3 The rule binds in law with automatic correction mechanism when exceeded.

### Article 2 — Escape clauses

2.1 Exceptions: natural disasters, emergencies under document 080, severe recession beyond the cycle adjustment - each declared, quantified and time-limited by parliamentary supermajority.

2.2 Repayment plans accompany every escape with fixed schedules.

2.3 War financing follows its own regime under document 235 with post-conflict consolidation plans.

### Article 3 — Transparency and control

3.1 The independent fiscal council validates assumptions and publishes compliance assessments with each budget.

3.2 Generational accounts and debt sustainability analyses are mandatory annexes.

3.3 Hidden liabilities - PPP, guarantees, pensions - are fully booked.

### Article 4 — Local discipline

4.1 Local capital budgets may borrow for investment within debt-service ratios under document 074.

4.2 Bailouts are statutory, conditional and penalise the borrowing decision-makers politically.

4.3 Consolidation support is available for hardship with reform conditions.

## Commentary

The debt brake is the German-Swiss instrument with the EU's failed experiences as calibration: strict structural ceiling, automatic correction, escape only for genuine catastrophes with repayment plans. Booking hidden liabilities kills the PPP-and-guarantee games through which deficits hid for decades. The fiscal council's independent validation is the difference between a rule and a suggestion.

## Notes for adopting states

- Fiscal rules require the independent fiscal council to be credible; appoint it before the first budget under the new regime.
- Tax reform moves as a package: base-broadening, pre-filling and compliance-gap publication together.
- The debt brake and the sovereign fund are intergenerational instruments; adopt them early, govern them forever.
