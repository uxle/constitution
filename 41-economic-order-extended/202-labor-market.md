# WC-202 · Labour Market and Full Employment

> **Part 08 — Economic Order** · Document **202 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The employment mandate: activation, flexicurity and shared prosperity

## Preamble

This document sets the labour-market policy framework of the corpus.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The mandate

1.1 The state pursues full employment as a policy objective with instruments: macro-stabilisation, activation, training and public investment.

1.2 Employment policy is coordinated by a tripartite council under document 149.

1.3 Statistics under the independent office measure performance honestly.

### Article 2 — Flexicurity

2.1 Flexible hiring under fair dismissal rules of document 148; generous income security under document 195; strong activation and training guarantees.

2.2 Transitions between jobs, sectors and regions are supported with mobility allowances and portability of entitlements.

2.3 Platform and gig work is regularised by the presumption of employment under document 017.

### Article 3 — Working time and sharing

3.1 The forty-hour default with collectively bargained variation; overtime premium; part-time equality of hourly terms.

3.2 Right to request flexibility with business-responsibility answers; fathers' quotas in parental leave.

3.3 Work-sharing in downturns is supported with short-time work schemes.

### Article 4 — Wages and productivity

4.1 Minimum wage commissions set floors annually with evidence from the productivity data.

4.2 Sectoral bargaining under document 149 coordinates wages with competitiveness.

4.3 Productivity policy: investment allowances, diffusion of technology to SMEs and workplace codetermination.

## Commentary

Flexicurity is the package that reconciles dynamic economies with security, and the chapter adopts its full logic rather than its slogans: flexible rules, real income insurance, hard activation. The employment mandate with honest statistics keeps unemployment a policy failure rather than an act of god. Work-sharing instruments exist because the alternative to short-time schemes was always layoffs first and rehiring never.
