# Section 20.4 — The Electoral Commission

> **Part 20 — Independent Institutions** · Articles 20.4.1 – 20.4.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section fixes the composition, qualifications, staggered terms, and removal rules of the Electoral Commission, the independent administrator of elections whose constitutional status is set in Part 03.*

## Article 20.4.1 — Composition and Staggered Terms

1. The Electoral Commission is a multi-member collegiate body; the number of members shall be fixed by law at not fewer than five.
2. Members are appointed for a single term of fixed length, staggered so that not all seats fall vacant at once and continuity is preserved.
3. The Commission's constitutional status and exclusive administration of national elections and referendums are governed by Article 3.5.1 and Article 3.5.3.

## Article 20.4.2 — Qualifications and Incompatibilities

1. Members shall be chosen for competence and integrity through an open, published process that includes an opportunity for public and parliamentary hearing.
2. Members may not hold party office, campaign for candidates, or hold any interest in the outcome of an election they administer.
3. Incompatibilities continue for a cooling-off period after the end of the term, as fixed by law.

## Article 20.4.3 — Removal Only for Cause

1. A member may be removed before the end of the term only for serious cause, namely incapacity, grave misconduct, or conviction of a serious offense, and only by a supermajority of the Parliament as fixed by law.
2. Removal decisions are subject to review by the courts, consistent with Article 5.6.5.
3. Suspension pending removal shall be exceptional and shall be justified in writing.

## Article 20.4.4 — Accountability and Cooperation

1. The Commission shall report annually to the Parliament and shall publish its regulations and its reasoned decisions on electoral disputes.
2. Public authorities shall cooperate with the Commission; interference with its functions shall be sanctioned by law, consistent with Article 3.5.4.

## Design Note

*Staggered single terms deny any government the chance to appoint a whole commission at once, which is the structural trick most often used to capture election administration. Cause-only removal by supermajority, open recruitment, and post-term cooling-off rules echo the good-practice standards for electoral management bodies developed by comparative election law. Preserving judicial review of removals ensures the supermajority is a shield for independence, not a weapon against it.*
