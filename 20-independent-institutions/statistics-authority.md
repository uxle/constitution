# Section 20.6 — The Statistics Authority

> **Part 20 — Independent Institutions** · Articles 20.6.1 – 20.6.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section guarantees the professional independence of official statistics, sets quality and confidentiality standards, and assigns the Statistics Authority the coordination of state data collection.*

## Article 20.6.1 — Professional Independence

1. The Statistics Authority shall produce official statistics free from political or commercial interference.
2. Decisions on statistical methods, sources, and the timing of release are made within the Authority alone; the release calendar is public and binding.
3. The head of the Authority serves a fixed term and may be removed only for cause by a supermajority of the Parliament.

## Article 20.6.2 — Quality and Standards

1. Official statistics shall be relevant, accurate, timely, coherent, and comparable, applying generally accepted statistical standards and classifications.
2. Methods and definitions shall be documented, published, and applied uniformly over time.

## Article 20.6.3 — Confidentiality of Statistical Data

1. Individual data collected for statistical purposes shall be used exclusively for those purposes and published only in aggregated or otherwise de-identified form.
2. Identifiable statistical data shall not be transferred to any organ of the State or used for administrative, investigative, or enforcement purposes.
3. Breaches of confidentiality shall be sanctioned by law, consistent with Article 1.5.4.

## Article 20.6.4 — Coordination of State Data Collection

1. The Authority shall coordinate statistical activities across public bodies to avoid duplication and reduce reporting burdens.
2. Public bodies shall provide administrative data to the Authority for statistical use under safeguards consistent with Article 20.6.3.
3. Aggregate official statistics shall be published openly by default, consistent with Article 21.3.2.
4. The Authority shall report annually to the Parliament on the state of official statistics.

## Design Note

*Independent statistics are an unglamorous but load-bearing institution: budgets, elections, and policy evaluation all rest on numbers the public must believe. The binding release calendar prevents the oldest abuse, the convenient delay or leak of bad figures, and reflects the principles of official statistical independence endorsed by the United Nations Statistical Commission. The strict firewall between statistical data and enforcement uses removes the fear that answering a survey becomes evidence against oneself.*
