# Section 20.3 — The Anti-Corruption Commission

> **Part 20 — Independent Institutions** · Articles 20.3.1 – 20.3.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section establishes the Anti-Corruption Commission to prevent and investigate corruption offenses, to prosecute jointly with the Public Prosecution Service, and to operate under strict independence guarantees.*

## Article 20.3.1 — Mandate

1. The Anti-Corruption Commission shall prevent and investigate corruption offenses, including bribery, trading in influence, embezzlement, and illicit enrichment, consistent with the criminal law of the Republic.
2. The Commission shall conduct prevention through education, risk assessment, and advice on integrity systems within public bodies.

## Article 20.3.2 — Joint Prosecution

1. Prosecution of corruption offenses shall be conducted jointly by the Commission and the Public Prosecution Service under the direction of the Public Prosecutor, consistent with Article 5.8.2.
2. The decision whether to prosecute shall follow the guidelines applicable to the Public Prosecution Service; no organ of the executive may direct it.

## Article 20.3.3 — Independence Guarantees

1. The Commission shall be independent in the exercise of its functions; no organ may direct or impede its investigations.
2. Its head and members serve fixed terms and may be removed only for cause by a qualified majority of the Parliament.
3. Its funding shall be adequate and may not be reduced in a manner designed to obstruct an investigation.

## Article 20.3.4 — Powers and Safeguards

1. The Commission may access documents, premises, and records, including banking records under judicial authorization.
2. Intrusive techniques require a judicial warrant on the ordinary standard of necessity and proportionality, consistent with Article 1.5.5.
3. Persons under investigation retain every procedural right available under the general law of criminal procedure.
4. The Commission shall publish an annual report on corruption trends and on the state of prevention within public bodies.

## Design Note

*Anti-corruption bodies succeed or fail on independence, so this Section entrenches fixed terms, cause-only removal, and protected funding. Combining investigative capacity with prosecution conducted jointly under the Public Prosecutor preserves a single chain of prosecutorial responsibility, a design chosen over fully autonomous prosecution powers to avoid fragmenting Article 5.8. Judicial warrants for the most intrusive powers keep the Commission's arsenal within the constitutional limits that bind every investigator.*
