# WC-089 · The Integrity Courts

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **089 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Specialised, fast, expert judicial track for corruption cases

## Preamble

This document establishes specialised courts for corruption and connected financial crime with expert judges, fast clocks and full transparency.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Establishment

1.1 Integrity Courts of first instance and an Integrity Appeals Chamber are part of the judiciary, staffed by judges selected for financial-crime expertise through the appointments path of document 069.

1.2 Judges serve staggered seven-year assignments in the track, renewable once, with the full independence guarantees of the judiciary.

1.3 Court staff include forensic accountants and data analysts under judicial direction.

### Article 2 — Jurisdiction

2.1 Jurisdiction covers corruption offences, unexplained-wealth cases, procurement fraud, recovery actions, electoral corruption offences, and obstruction of integrity institutions.

2.2 Confiscation and recovery proceedings may run in parallel with criminal cases.

2.3 Conflicts with ordinary jurisdiction are resolved by the joint senate of the apex courts.

### Article 3 — Speed and transparency

3.1 Indictment to trial within twelve months; judgment within six months of closing arguments; breaches are reported to the judicial council.

3.2 Proceedings are public with witness protection exceptions; all judgments are published with reasons, including asset tables.

3.3 Reasons state why the court departed, if it did, from sentencing guidelines.

### Article 4 — Supervision powers

4.1 Integrity Courts confirm seizures, freezes and passport orders, and review investigation legitimacy within forty-eight hours of complaint.

4.2 They supervise corporate compliance monitors and can void contracts procured by corruption under document 148.

4.3 Their structural orders - deadlines on institutions - carry contempt escalation under document 067.

## Commentary

Specialised anti-corruption chambers have shown the speed and expertise advantage in Slovakia, Kenya's anti-corruption divisions and the UK's economic-crime courts; the corpus adopts the model while keeping judges inside the ordinary judicial career to protect quality. Financial expertise of staff matters as much as judges, because forensic accounting is where these trials are won. Publication of asset tables with judgments builds the public record that makes recovery socially credible.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
