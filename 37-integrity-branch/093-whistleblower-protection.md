# WC-093 · Whistleblower Protection and Rewards

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **093 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The sensor network of integrity: protection, compensation and rewards

## Preamble

This document protects whistleblowers absolutely and pays them for value delivered, turning every official into a potential integrity sensor.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Protected disclosures

1.1 Any person who, in good faith and on reasonable belief, reports corruption, illegality, danger to public health or safety, fraud or cover-up, is protected against dismissal, demotion, harassment, blacklisting and any retaliation, in public or private employment.

1.2 Protection attaches at disclosure, survives leaving the job, and extends to witnesses and helpers.

1.3 Anonymous channels preserve protection; identity is revealed only with consent or sealed court order.

### Article 2 — Reverse burden

2.1 In retaliation cases, the employer bears the burden of proving that adverse action was unrelated to the disclosure.

2.2 Interim reinstatement is available within fourteen days pending the case.

2.3 Remedies: reinstatement, full compensation including career-loss, aggravated damages for malicious retaliation, and criminal sanction for retaliators in office.

### Article 3 — Rewards

3.1 Where a disclosure leads to recovery or verified savings, the whistleblower receives a statutory share of ten to thirty percent by decision of the Integrity Courts, capped and taxed as ordinary income.

3.2 Rewards are paid before treasury transfer, within ninety days of realisation.

3.3 Bad-faith or fabricated reporting forfeits protection and constitutes the offence of false accusation.

### Article 4 — Channels

4.1 Protected channels exist inside every employer above fifty staff, with the Integrity Commission as universal external channel, accepting anonymous reports in all languages.

4.2 Feedback on report status is owed within fifteen days; investigation updates follow at statutory intervals.

4.3 Gagging clauses, NDAs and settlement terms that suppress crime reporting are void.

## Commentary

Whistleblowers are the single most productive source of corruption discovery, ahead of audits and media; the design protects them with the reverse burden and interim reinstatement that made US and French systems functional, and pays them on the US False Claims Act model, which turned qui tam reporting into an industry of integrity. Voiding suppressive NDAs closes the private exit through which organisations buy silence. Feedback duties within fifteen days are the cheap fix for the trust problem: channels without answers train people not to report.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
