# WC-106 · International Cooperation and Asset Recovery

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 106/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Chasing stolen wealth across borders with speed and standing

## Preamble

This document builds the international machinery: fast MLA, non-conviction-based return, and standing for victim states.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Fast mutual legal assistance

1.1 MLA requests for corruption, fraud and laundering are executed within ninety days through a central authority staffed by the Integrity Commission and the Justice Ministry jointly.

1.2 Bank secrecy, tax confidentiality and corporate secrecy do not block identification, freezing and evidence transfer for corruption offences.

1.3 Urgent freezes execute within forty-eight hours through the Egmont-group financial intelligence channel.

### Article 2 — Non-conviction-based return

2.1 Assets are returned to victim states and victims on civil standards where conviction is impossible by death, flight or immunity, following document 092.

2.2 Comity and human-rights safeguards protect bona fide third parties.

2.3 Return agreements are published; deducted costs are itemised.

### Article 3 — Victim-state standing

3.1 Foreign states may sue in domestic courts to recover looted assets, with equal standing and no national-security carve-out.

3.2 Limitation runs from knowledge of the theft abroad.

3.3 Settlements require publication and victim consultation.

### Article 4 — Foreign bribes and laundering

4.1 Bribery of foreign officials by nationals or residents is prosecutable as domestic bribery, with no double-criminality barrier.

4.2 Laundering definitions cover proceeds of foreign corruption and self-laundering.

4.3 The state recognises foreign confiscation orders and enforces them after rights review.

## Commentary

Stolen public money travels; recovery regimes historically did not, and the corpus adopts the strongest instruments - civil standards, no bank-secrecy barrier, standing for victim states - because kleptocracy is transnational by design. Prosecuting foreign official bribery without double-criminality follows the OECD convention's logic to its end point. Publishing return agreements with itemised costs answers the documented abuse where recovery became a fee business for intermediaries.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
