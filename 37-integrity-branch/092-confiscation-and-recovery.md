# WC-092 · Confiscation and Domestic Recovery

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 092/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Taking the profit out of corruption, fully and by default

## Preamble

This document makes confiscation automatic, comprehensive and victim-restoring, so that corruption is never a rational investment.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Automatic confiscation

1.1 Conviction of a corruption offence carries mandatory confiscation of the proceeds, benefits and instrumentalities, without judicial discretion to waive.

1.2 Equivalent-value confiscation applies where proceeds are dissipated, transformed or hidden.

1.3 Third-party transfers in bad faith or without value are set aside.

### Article 2 — Civil and non-conviction based

2.1 Where prosecution is impossible by death, flight or immunity, civil recovery proceeds against assets on the balance of probabilities.

2.2 Preventive freezing within forty-eight hours of charge application preserves the fund.

2.3 Management of frozen assets is professional, costed and reported; depreciation is a recoverable fault of the managing agency.

### Article 3 — Distribution

3.1 Recovery priority: victims of the corrupt act; the public treasury; restitution to affected institutions.

3.2 A published recovery report names amounts, cases and destinations annually.

3.3 No recovery fund may be spent without budget-line transparency.

### Article 4 — Corporate assets

4.1 Confiscation extends to corporate proceeds and to profits of shareholders who knew, constructive knowledge included for control persons.

4.2 Compliance programmes reduce individual culpability, never the confiscation of proceeds.

4.3 Dissolution of repeat-offender firms follows the corporate-crime rules of document 138.

## Commentary

The economics of deterrence live in recovery, not in prison: as long as expected profit exceeds expected sanction times probability, corruption pays. Mandatory confiscation with equivalent-value powers removes the profit line of the ledger entirely. Priority to victims converts recovery from a treasury windfall into a justice instrument, which sustains public cooperation; published destination reports prevent the recycling of recovered loot into discretionary funds, an abuse documented in several systems.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
