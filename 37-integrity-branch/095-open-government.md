# WC-095 · Open Government and Freedom of Information

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 095/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Public information by default with narrow, logged exceptions

## Preamble

This document establishes the radical-openness regime: every public document releasable unless law says no, with the presumption resolving for release.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The principle

1.1 All information held by public bodies is public, unless exempted by a specific statute naming a protectable interest.

1.2 Exemptions are narrow, harm-tested, time-limited and never class-wide: national security narrowly, personal privacy, ongoing investigations, and cabinet deliberation for a maximum of ten years.

1.3 Commercial confidentiality may never shield public contracts, prices or performance data.

### Article 2 — The right

2.1 Any person requests any document without stating reasons, identity or nationality; answers issue within fifteen working days, extensions reasoned and appealable.

2.2 Partial release is the rule; blackening is documented with exemption grounds per redaction.

2.3 The public-interest override forces release where the public benefit outweighs the exemption, always for corruption and abuse information.

### Article 3 — Proactive publication

3.1 Registers, budgets, contracts, subsidies, permits, statistics, laws and their drafts are published proactively in open, machine-readable formats, free of charge.

3.2 The open-data catalogue follows the common formats of document 165 with per-dataset owners and update duties.

3.3 FOI performance - volumes, response times, refusal grounds - is reported quarterly per body.

### Article 4 — Records law

4.1 Every significant public act is recorded and archived; private communication channels for public business are prohibited, and auto-deletion of public records is a criminal offence.

4.2 Archives transfer to openness on statutory schedules of thirty years, ten for decision files.

4.3 Destruction of records during investigations is the aggravated offence of document 108.

## Commentary

Sunlight is the classic disinfectant, and the corpus writes the strongest version of it: no reason for asking, harm-tested exemptions, class-wide secrecy forbidden, and the public-interest override with teeth for corruption information. The records clauses matter as much as release: corruption migrates to private messaging apps precisely where archives do not reach, so off-channel public business is banned, and auto-deletion becomes a crime rather than an oops.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
