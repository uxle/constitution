# WC-087 · Investigative Powers and Safeguards

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **087 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The full toolkit with judges, clocks and remedies for abuse

## Preamble

This document grants the Commission the complete investigative toolkit of a specialised police force, under judicial control and strict time limits.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The toolkit

1.1 Investigators hold the powers of judicial police: identification, summons, interrogation with counsel, search, seizure, arrest, technical and financial surveillance, controlled deliveries and covert operations for corruption offences.

1.2 Bank, tax, customs, telecom and company data are produced on Commission order confirmed by a judge within seventy-two hours.

1.3 Cross-border evidence follows the fast MLA gateways of document 105.

### Article 2 — Judicial control

2.1 Covert measures require the specialised warrant of document 027; seizures and freezes are confirmed by the Integrity Courts within forty-eight hours.

2.2 Interrogations are recorded; minors, and witnesses in fear, receive special protections.

2.3 The duty of candour applies to investigators: exculpatory material is preserved and disclosed.

### Article 3 — Clocks

3.1 Preliminary inquiry concludes within ninety days; investigation within two years, extendable once by the Integrity Courts for complex financial cases.

3.2 Charging decisions issue within sixty days of file closure.

3.3 Delays are reported monthly and breaches sanctionable.

### Article 4 — Remedies for abuse

4.1 Targets may complain to the Integrity Courts; unlawful evidence is excluded under document 031; malicious investigation yields compensation and officer discipline.

4.2 The Citizens' Oversight Assembly reviews closed files where no charge followed for patterns of targeting.

4.3 Publishing targets' names before charge is prohibited; leaks are offences.

## Commentary

Powerful investigators without safeguards become the corruption they hunt, so every power sits inside a judicial confirmation and a statutory clock. The ninety-day inquiry limit exists because the 'preliminary investigation' is the classic freezer where inconvenient politicians are parked forever. Conversely, the rights of targets are real: pre-charge anonymity with leak offences prevents the weaponisation of investigation as media punishment, a technique documented in multiple jurisdictions.

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
