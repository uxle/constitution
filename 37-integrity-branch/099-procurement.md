# WC-099 · Public Procurement

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 099/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Open contracting: the single largest corruption channel, closed

## Preamble

This document governs the purchase of everything by the state: e-procurement, open data, integrity filters and debarment.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Open by construction

1.1 All procurement above thresholds is run on a single national e-procurement system: one portal, one machine-readable pipeline, public at every stage.

1.2 Tenders, specifications, questions, answers, bids, evaluation scores, contracts, amendments, payments and performance are published in the standard open-contracting data structure.

1.3 Only genuinely narrow exceptions - narrow security grounds confirmed by a judge - allow unpublished awards, and the exception itself is published.

### Article 2 — Design rules

2.1 Splitting to avoid thresholds is void; framework and central-purchasing bodies aggregate for scale.

2.2 Specifications are outcome-based, neutral to brands, and designed by officers without supplier contact; tailored specs that fit one bidder trigger automatic review.

2.3 Evaluation criteria and weights are fixed and published before offers; scoring sheets are individual, signed and published.

### Article 3 — Integrity filters

3.1 Bidders declare beneficial ownership, conflicts, prior debarments and subcontracts; declarations are verified through the register of document 093.

3.2 Conflict-screening blocks officials with ties to bidders from the process; assignment is logged.

3.3 The Commission runs a market-watch analytics unit: single-bidder rates, price dispersion, amendment and cost-overrun patterns, publication lag, and cartel-signature tests, publishing quarterly risk reports by agency.

### Article 4 — Debarment and remedies

4.1 Corruption, cartel conduct and fraud convictions debar the firm and control persons for ten years; systemic breaches for five.

4.2 Debarment lists are public with cross-recognition under international agreements.

4.3 Losing bidders challenge awards before the administrative courts in a standstill window of fifteen days with fast decision in thirty.

## Commentary

Procurement is where between ten and thirty percent of public investment is typically lost to corruption, so the regime is designed as an open pipeline rather than a set of prohibitions: publication by architecture, as in Ukraine's ProZorro, where visibility is the enforcement. Analytics-based market watch follows the most productive modern supervision, because cartels and tailored specifications are found in data patterns, not complaints. The standstill challenge window is the small procedural detail that makes the whole system justiciable in time to matter.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
