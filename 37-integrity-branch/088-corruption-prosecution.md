# WC-088 · Prosecution of Corruption

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 088/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** From file to verdict: charging, cooperation and appeal

## Preamble

This document governs the prosecution of corruption cases: charging criteria, cooperation deals, transparency and appeal discipline.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Charging

1.1 The Prosecution Division charges where the evidence would justify conviction, applying published charging criteria; declinations are reasoned and reviewable by the Integrity Courts on complaint.

1.2 Every charging decision is logged with reasons; selective non-prosecution is an offence.

1.3 Statutes of limitation for corruption run from discovery, never from the act, and toll during any obstruction.

### Article 2 — Cooperation and settlement

2.1 Cooperation agreements with minor participants require full disclosure, asset surrender and judicial approval with published reasons in anonymised form.

2.2 Deferred prosecutions of corporations are available with independent monitors, published terms and victim restitution before shareholder distributions.

2.3 Immunity of a principal offender requires the Integrity Courts' consent, a written justification and publication in aggregate annually.

### Article 3 — No negotiation on the essential

3.1 Charge bargaining away corruption offences or asset forfeiture in full is prohibited.

3.2 Sentences below statutory minimums for corruption require written judicial reasons published with the judgment.

3.3 Political or diplomatic pressure on prosecutors is the obstruction offence of document 108.

### Article 4 — Appeals

4.1 Both sides may appeal on law and fact to the Integrity Appeals Chamber.

4.2 Acquittals are appealable where new evidence emerges; no double jeopardy without strict novelty, under document 031.

4.3 Retrials after appellate reversal follow the milder-law rule.

## Commentary

Prosecution is where anti-corruption systems are usually strangled politely: declinations without reasons, endless cooperation deals, and settlements without monitors. Each is addressed: reasoned declinations, judicially-approved cooperation with published logic, and the prohibition of bargaining away forfeiture, which was the standard hole in plea practice. Limitation running from discovery follows modern anti-corruption conventions, because complex schemes are designed to surface after limitation would otherwise have run.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
