# WC-086 · The Preventive Mandate

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **086 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Corruption-proofing systems before money moves

## Preamble

This document gives the Commission the mandate to redesign systems so that corruption becomes structurally difficult, not merely punishable.

## Articles

### Article 1 — System audits

1.1 The Prevention Division audits high-risk systems - procurement, permits, licensing, subsidies, taxes, customs, land administration - on a published rolling plan and on Integrity-Court order after any systemic finding.

1.2 Audit standards are international and published; conclusions are public with management responses.

1.3 Re-audit within eighteen months is mandatory for every critical finding.

### Article 2 — Corruption proofing of bills

2.1 Every bill affecting money, permits or discretion carries a corruption-risk assessment prepared by the Commission, annexed before the second reading.

2.2 The assessment identifies discretion points, single-officer chokepoints, information asymmetries and conflict risks, and proposes controls.

2.3 Parliament departing from the assessment states reasons in the committee report.

### Article 3 — Process redesign

3.1 Where a system produces repeated findings, the Commission may impose design duties: dual control, random assignment, service charters with deadlines, digital queues and audit trails.

3.2 Agencies implement within statutory deadlines or face the follow-up regime of document 077.

3.3 Redesigns are validated by red-team exercises that attempt simulated corruption.

### Article 4 — Education and culture

4.1 The Education Division delivers mandatory annual training to all officials, school curricula modules, and public campaigns with measurable outcome indicators.

4.2 Integrity training completion is a condition of delegation of financial authority under document 066.

4.3 The Commission publishes an annual Integrity Index of institutions under document 290.

## Commentary

Prevention is where the zero in zero-corruption is actually earned: prosecution alone yields scandals, systems yield integrity. Corruption-risk assessment of bills follows the practice pioneered in Korea and adopted in several Eastern European states, and its cost is trivial against the damage of a single captured law. The red-team validation of redesigns borrows from security practice, because control systems that are never attacked cannot be said to resist attack. The Integrity Index creates public competitive pressure that moves institutions more reliably than sanctions do.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
