# WC-102 · Immunity Restricted for Corruption

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 102/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** No shield of office against integrity investigation

## Preamble

This document strips immunity for corruption offences at every level while preserving functional protections for votes and opinions.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The principle

1.1 No immunity of any office - political, diplomatic, parliamentary, judicial - blocks investigation, search, arrest or prosecution of corruption offences.

1.2 Functional immunity for votes and opinions follows document 082 unchanged.

1.3 The fast-track lifting procedure of document 082 applies, with the thirty-day Supreme Court opinion and the sixty-day chamber decision.

### Article 2 — Sitting officials

2.1 The President, ministers and parliamentarians are chargeable without the ordinary preliminary motions where the Integrity Courts certify probable cause on the evidence.

2.2 Pre-trial measures for sitting officials are decided by the Integrity Courts directly, preserving the separation from political bodies.

2.3 Court certification requires documented acts, not political accusation, and is appealable immediately.

### Article 3 — Diplomatic and international

3.1 Diplomatic immunity does not cover active or passive bribery, laundering or recovery actions; the sending state's waiver is presumed requested by treaty.

3.2 Head-of-state immunity abroad excludes international crimes including grand corruption under document 139.

3.3 Official acts defence is unavailable for corruption: corruption is never an official function.

### Article 4 — After office

4.1 Protection ends with the office; prosecution for acts in office is time-barred only by the long limitation of document 085, running from discovery.

4.2 Amnesties and pardons for corruption are void under document 064.

4.3 Successor governments may not extinguish pending corruption cases of predecessors.

## Commentary

Functional immunity protects democracy; criminal immunity protects criminals, and the corpus separates the two cleanly. Certification by Integrity Courts rather than political bodies answers both the abuse risk of political prosecutions and the abuse risk of political shields, keeping the gate judicial and evidence-based. Voiding amnesties and pardons for corruption closes the documented pipelines of self-protection that leaders carved into their exit strategies in multiple systems.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
