# WC-090 · Asset and Interest Disclosure

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **090 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Public registers of wealth for everyone who exercises public power

## Preamble

This document imposes universal, public, verified asset and interest disclosure on every person who exercises public power, and their close circles.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Who discloses

1.1 Disclosure is mandatory for: elected officials at every level; ministers and political appointees; judges, prosecutors and senior investigators; generals and police commanders; chief executives and boards of state enterprises and agencies; procurement officials above thresholds; tax, customs and land officials; and candidates for any of these offices before nomination is valid.

1.2 Disclosure covers spouse, partner and dependent children, and beneficial interests held by intermediaries or relatives.

1.3 Failure to file blocks entry into office; false filing voids appointment.

### Article 2 — What is disclosed

2.1 Assets: real property, vehicles, watercraft, aircraft, securities, bank and cash holdings above one year's salary, business ownership, beneficial stakes, debts, and liabilities; and income sources by category.

2.2 Interests: board positions, outside employment, gifts above nominal value from any source, memberships of bodies, and relevant contracts.

2.3 Cryptographic wallets and digital assets are included and traceable by address.

### Article 3 — Public and verified

3.1 Registers are public online in open data, searchable by name and office, with privacy-safe redaction of home addresses.

3.2 The Commission verifies a risk-based sample of at least twenty percent annually, plus every declaration flagged by analytics, complaints or press.

3.3 Every verification result is published: consistent, inconsistent-under-explanation, or referred for investigation.

### Article 4 — Lifecycle

4.1 Declarations are filed on entry, annually by a fixed calendar date, on promotion, and on exit; exit declarations freeze monitoring for five years of subsequent change.

4.2 Unexplained variation between declarations triggers the wealth procedure of document 089.

4.3 Retention for thirty years in the public archive.

## Commentary

Public, verified and universal disclosure is the strongest single transparency device, and the design choices are deliberately aggressive: pre-nomination validity blocks the run-for-immunity trick, family coverage blocks the proxy-holding trick, and exit declarations with five-year monitoring block the cash-out-on-retirement trick. The twenty percent risk-based verification with publication of every result turns the register from a filing exercise into a live detection instrument, and the analytics of the Data Office feed it continuously.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
