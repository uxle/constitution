# WC-091 · Unexplained Wealth Jurisdiction

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **091 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The reversal of the burden for unjustifiable enrichment of officials

## Preamble

This document creates civil unexplained-wealth orders and their criminal counterpart, shifting the burden to officials whose wealth outruns their lawful income.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The order

1.1 Where an official subject to disclosure holds assets disproportionate to lawful income, the Integrity Commission may apply for an Unexplained Wealth Order before the Integrity Courts.

1.2 The order requires the respondent to explain the acquisition with evidence; the standard is the balance of probabilities.

1.3 The claim is against the asset, not only the person: death or flight does not extinguish it.

### Article 2 — Triggers

2.1 Triggers are analytical (declaration variance), transactional (cash patterns, luxury purchases), investigative (connected persons), or external (credible reports).

2.2 Third parties holding assets for officials are joined to the procedure with the same burden.

2.3 Fictional loans, gambling or gift narratives require documentary proof, not testimony alone.

### Article 3 — Effect and sanction

3.1 Unexplained assets are confiscated to the treasury; deliberate false explanation adds the criminal offence of obstruction with aggravated corruption grading.

3.2 Confiscation is without prejudice to criminal prosecution, which may follow on the same facts.

3.3 Wrongly-issued orders are compensated on proof of lawful origin with costs.

### Article 4 — Constitutional calibration

4.1 The reversed burden applies only to disclosure-subject officials, only to assets acquired during office or one year before, and only before an independent court - not an agency.

4.2 Proceedings are public; judgments publish the full asset table.

4.3 The guarantee against self-incrimination is preserved: silence in the criminal file cannot alone ground conviction, but the civil confiscation may proceed on the unexplained balance.

## Commentary

Unexplained wealth provisions are the most litigated and most valuable instruments in modern anti-corruption law; the corpus adopts them with the calibration that survives constitutional review: officials only, assets from the office period, independent courts, and a genuine opportunity to explain. The in-rem character defeats the classic escape of dying or fleeing with the loot. Pairing civil confiscation with criminal exposure keeps the incentive structure honest: explanation is possible, but fabricated explanations are a new offence.

- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
