# WC-104 · Anti-Nepotism and Cronyism

> **Part 37 — Integrity Branch** · Source: World Constitution v1.0 corpus, document 104/300 (adapted) · This edition v1.0.1
> [README](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Subject.** Merit in public jobs and contracts, family and friendship aside

## Preamble

This document abolishes the private staffing of the state: hiring, appointments and contracts by merit, with family and political ties excluded.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Public employment

1.1 Public jobs are filled by the competitive rules of document 065; hiring relatives, partners or household members into one's office, supervision or budget line is prohibited.

1.2 Career acceleration, postings and perks for relatives of officials are void acts with disciplinary consequence.

1.3 Political appointments are confined to the capped private offices of document 063 with public job descriptions.

### Article 2 — Public contracts

2.1 Contracts to family members or their companies are void under the procurement rules; the single-source exception may never cover them.

2.2 Friends, party donors and campaign staff follow the first-year conflict block of document 097.

2.3 Beneficial-ownership checks reveal hidden family ties automatically.

### Article 3 — Boards and honours

3.1 Appointments to state boards, agencies and honours are made by open competition with published criteria; the spoils system is unconstitutional.

3.2 Ambassadorships, regulatory posts and SOE chairs are merit posts, never rewards.

3.3 Appointment files with scores are public for senior posts.

### Article 4 — Enforcement

4.1 Nepotism is a corruption-grade integrity offence for the patron and a void act for the beneficiary's position.

4.2 Salaries paid under void hires are recoverable from both.

4.3 The Commission audits hiring patterns by kinship analytics across agencies annually.

## Commentary

Nepotism is the entry-level corruption through which citizens experience the state as someone else's family business, and it is the recruiting ground for grander capture. Making void the hire and recoverable the salary converts it from a free option into a costly exposure. Kinship analytics sounds intrusive but runs on declaration cross-matches that are already public, and it detects the patterns that individual complaints never see.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
