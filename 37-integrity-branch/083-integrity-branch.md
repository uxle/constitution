# WC-083 · The Integrity Branch

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **083 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The fourth power: mission, structure and constitutional armour

## Preamble

This document establishes the Integrity Branch as a co-equal branch with the mission of zero tolerance of corruption, and arms it against every known form of political retaliation.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Mission

1.1 The mission of the Integrity Branch is to achieve and sustain a state of zero tolerance of corruption: prevention by design, detection by data, investigation without fear, prosecution without delay, and recovery without compromise.

1.2 Corruption means the abuse of entrusted power for private gain, in the public or private sector, as defined in document 108.

1.3 The Branch serves the people, not the government of the day.

### Article 2 — Structure

2.1 The Branch comprises: the Integrity Commission, with prevention, education, investigation and prosecution divisions; the Integrity Courts; the regional integrity offices; and the Citizens' Oversight Assembly.

2.2 The Auditor-General cooperates under the gateway of document 075.

2.3 The Branch's jurisdiction covers all branches, all levels, all public money, and the private sector where it touches public functions.

### Article 3 — Constitutional armour

3.1 The Branch is a branch of the state; its existence, mandate and budget protection cannot be reduced by ordinary statute, only by the full amendment procedure with the unamendable-core guarantee of document 008.

3.2 No emergency, war, or caretaker period suspends the Branch; its powers expand to emergency procurement automatically under document 080.

3.3 Attacks on the Branch - defunding, staffing freezes, hostile reorganisation, criminalising its lawful methods - are high constitutional offences.

### Article 4 — Duty to act

4.1 The Branch shall register and assess every corruption complaint within fifteen days and communicate its decision to the complainant.

4.2 Declining to investigate a credible complaint requires written reasons, appealable to the Integrity Courts.

4.3 The Branch may not practise deliberate slowness: statutory clocks apply to every procedural stage.

### Article 5 — Self-integrity

5.1 The Branch is itself under the strongest regime: its officials disclose assets annually and at every promotion, its files are audited by the Auditor-General, and its closed cases are reviewed by the Citizens' Oversight Assembly.

5.2 Commission investigators are rotated across sectors and regions on statutory cycles.

5.3 Corruption inside the Branch carries aggravated penalties under document 108.

## Commentary

Every known anti-corruption agency has faced the same two threats: capture by the executive it is meant to police, and erosion by budget and statute. This document answers both with branch rank, amendment-level protection, and automatic expansion of jurisdiction in emergencies, where corruption historically peaks. The self-integrity regime - asset disclosure, external audit, citizen review of closed cases - is deliberately stricter than for any other institution, because the Branch's only asset is credibility. The duty to act with clocks and appealable refusals prevents the quiet pathology of agencies that bury complaints in procedural amber.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
