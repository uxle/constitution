# WC-094 · Journalists' Source Protection

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **094 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** The press as integrity institution: sources, seizures and surveillance

## Preamble

This document protects journalism's information supply chain so that exposure of corruption remains possible when institutions fail.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The core

1.1 No journalist shall be compelled to reveal a source or hand over material gathered for journalism, save by the strict three-part court test of document 019.

1.2 Seizures in newsrooms require judicial authorisation, executed with independent counsel present; material unrelated to the warrant is sealed.

1.3 Surveillance of journalists, their metadata or their families by any authority is the criminal offence of obstruction of press freedom.

### Article 2 — Data protection for sources

2.1 Newsroom data enjoys heightened protection: no bulk seizure, no cloud grabs without the same warrant, and encryption tools for journalists are lawful and protected.

2.2 Platform data requests touching journalists go through the journalistic-material gateway with prior notice and challenge opportunity.

2.3 Device searches at borders are limited to copy-and-seal under judicial control, never to rummage.

### Article 3 — Anti-SLAPP

3.1 Vexatious lawsuits against journalism on public-interest matters are dismissed early with costs against the plaintiff; repeat abusive claimants pay treble costs.

3.2 Judges certify claims as abusive on motion of the defendant, media councils or the Ombudsman.

3.3 Judgments report abusive-filing patterns to the Integrity Commission.

### Article 4 — Cooperation

4.1 The Integrity Commission operates a journalism liaison: leaked material is received with source-protection guarantees, evaluated and, if corroborated, converted into investigations.

4.2 Journalists are never compelled to become witnesses in cases arising from their reporting, save the general three-part test.

4.3 Killings, threats and harassment of journalists are priority offences under document 128.

## Commentary

Investigative journalism is the integrity system of last resort, and its infrastructure fails quietly through surveillance, seizures and lawsuits rather than censorship. Anti-SLAPP rules follow the EU directive and the strongest provincial practice, because litigation is the modern instrument to bury exposés. The liaison channel acknowledges reality: most major corruption scandals began with a reporter, and the state's duty is to protect the channel, not to capture it.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
