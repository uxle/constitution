# WC-096 · Beneficial Ownership Transparency

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **096 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Ending anonymous companies: who owns what, publicly

## Preamble

This document abolishes anonymous corporate ownership and makes every owner of every company publicly traceable.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The register

1.1 Every legal person registered or operating in the state files a verified beneficial-ownership declaration identifying natural persons holding or controlling above ten percent, voting control, or management-control positions.

1.2 The register is public, machine-readable, free, and searchable by person and company; banks, notaries, procurement bodies and land registries use it under law.

1.3 Filing is a condition of incorporation, property registration, contracting with the state, and opening bank accounts.

### Article 2 — Verification and accuracy

2.1 Registrars verify declarations against identity documents, cross-check with company, tax, land and customs data, and publish confidence flags.

2.2 Discrepancies between the register and data held by regulated professionals are reported duties for banks and notaries.

2.3 Stale declarations older than one year block new filings and state contracts.

### Article 3 — Foreign and complex structures

3.1 Foreign entities doing business or holding assets must disclose their ownership chains to natural persons, up to the top; trusts and foundations file equivalent declarations.

3.2 Nominee arrangements are lawful only with registered, published declarations of the beneficial owner.

3.3 Non-compliant foreign structures may not buy land, bid for contracts, or sue in the courts.

### Article 4 — Sanctions

4.1 False declaration is a corruption-grade offence for the declarer and the nominee; the company is struck and its assets frozen pending true ownership.

4.2 Professional enablers - notaries, lawyers, accountants - who knowingly construct anonymity are charged as participants.

4.3 Privacy of natural persons is protected by address redaction only, never by name shielding.

## Commentary

Anonymous companies are the freight system of corruption: bribes, looted assets and sanctions evasion all travel in bearer-structure vehicles. The register design follows the strongest practice - free, public, verified, machine-readable - and closes the foreign-entity hole through which domestic registries are bypassed. Striking non-compliant companies from access to land, courts and contracts converts transparency from a filing form into a licence to operate.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
