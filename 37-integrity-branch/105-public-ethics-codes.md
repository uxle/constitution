# WC-105 · Public Ethics Codes

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **105 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** One code for all officeholders, statutory and sanctioned

## Preamble

This document creates the single statutory ethics code for every officeholder in the state, with common sanctions and one referee.

## Articles

### Article 1 — One code

1.1 A single Public Ethics Code applies to elected officials, ministers, advisers, civil servants, judges, military, police, SOE officers and contractors performing public functions.

1.2 The code sets minimum standards; institutions may add stricter, never weaker rules.

1.3 The code is law: it is enacted by organic statute and may not be amended by internal circular.

### Article 2 — Contents

2.1 The code fixes: the duties of documents 066; the disclosure duties of document 090; conflict management of document 097; the gift and door rules of document 099; political neutrality in service delivery; and courtesy standards toward citizens.

2.2 Plain-language guides, case examples and advisory opinions accompany the code.

2.3 Advisory opinions protect officials who act in line with them.

### Article 3 — Referee

3.1 The Integrity Commission administers the code: advisory opinions, investigations, and referrals to the disciplinary councils or the Integrity Courts.

3.2 Disciplinary councils sit with independent chairs under document 066.

3.3 Sanctions are published quarterly in anonymised aggregate.

### Article 4 — Compliance systems

4.1 Employers above fifty staff run internal channels, training and risk registers under the whistleblower regime.

4.2 Compliance failures are attributed to leadership, not only to juniors.

4.3 Certified compliance is a condition for public contracts of major value.

## Commentary

Fragmented codes are how standards get gamed: one ministry strict, a state company lax. A single statutory code with one referee and common sanctions ends forum shopping between regimes. The advisory-opinion mechanism is the undervalued gem of modern ethics administration: it converts the code from a trap into guidance, protects good-faith officials, and generates the case law that makes standards concrete.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
