# WC-101 · Gifts, Hospitality and the Revolving Door

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **101 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Zero gifts, and a five-year lock on cashing office into private jobs

## Preamble

This document enforces the zero-gift rule and the revolving-door cooling-off regime with void contracts and two-sided liability.

## Articles

### Article 1 — Zero threshold

1.1 Officeholders accept no gifts, hospitality, travel, discounts or advantages connected to their function, above nominal courtesy value fixed at one month's minimum wage.

1.2 Nominal courtesies are logged and published quarterly; everything else is refused or surrendered to the state.

1.3 Family members and staff are covered by the same rule through the officeholder.

### Article 2 — Sponsored travel

2.1 Official travel is publicly funded; junkets, conference sponsorships and study tours by private interests are prohibited.

2.2 Academic invitations with public academic programmes follow the rules of the host institution, logged and published.

2.3 Secret diplomatic or corporate travel is an offence for both sides.

### Article 3 — The revolving door

3.1 Senior officials and ministers may not take employment, mandates or consultancies with entities they regulated, contracted or supervised for five years; procurement officials for five years on their files; for seven years where they granted licences of major value.

3.2 Approval of exceptions is by the Integrity Commission with publication.

3.3 Golden handshakes and bonuses conditioned on public decisions are bribes.

### Article 4 — Enforcement

4.1 Violations are offences for both the giver and the receiver; the private party is barred from public contracts for ten years.

4.2 Contracts closed through door-traffic within the ban are voidable.

4.3 The register of departures and destinations is public for all senior officials.

## Commentary

The zero-gift rule with a low nominal threshold follows the Singaporean clean-civil-service logic: the gift economy starts small, and its first instalment is the social hook of later capture. Two-sided liability for the private party matters because firms calculate the price of capture as a business cost; making it a ten-year procurement ban changes the arithmetic. The published departure register lets journalists and the Commission watch the door in real time, which is where enforcement actually happens.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
