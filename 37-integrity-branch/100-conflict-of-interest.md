# WC-100 · Conflict of Interest

> **Part 04 — Integrity Branch - Zero Corruption** · Document **100 / 300** · World Constitution (WC) v1.0
> [Index](../INDEX.md) · [README](../README.md) · [Sources](../SOURCES.md)

**Subject.** Public duty first: disclosure, recusal and management

## Preamble

This document defines conflicts of interest for every officeholder and imposes the machinery of disclosure, recusal and sanction.

## Articles

### Article 1 — The rule

1.1 A conflict exists where a personal, family, financial or political interest could improperly influence, or appear to influence, the exercise of public duty.

1.2 The appearance standard applies: what a reasonable citizen would think matters.

1.3 Conflicts are managed, never hidden; disclosure is owed at the moment of knowledge, before acting.

### Article 2 — Management

2.1 Disclose to the superior, ethics officer or commission; recuse from decisions, files and negotiations; screens and divestments where recusal is impossible.

2.2 Recused officials do not attend, advise or receive information on the matter.

2.3 Blind trusts are the standard instrument for portfolios of ongoing financial interest, with certified entry and exit.

### Article 3 — Family and post-employment

3.1 Hiring of relatives in one's office or supervision chain is prohibited; relatives may not be contracted by the office.

3.2 Post-employment conflicts follow the cooling-off regime of document 099.

3.3 Campaign staff and donors are conflicted from procurement and appointments in the winner's administration for its first year.

### Article 4 — Sanctions

4.1 Non-disclosure and conflicted action are integrity offences; acts taken in conflict are voidable at the instance of the state.

4.2 Personal gain obtained through conflict is confiscated under document 092.

4.3 Repeated conflicts are presumptive corruption under document 108.

## Commentary

Conflict rules are the constitutional hygiene of public power, and their failure mode is always the same: the small exception, the family hire, the helpful meeting. The article makes the appearance standard explicit, which is the doctrinal core that keeps the rule usable, and it makes conflicted acts voidable - a remedy with deterrent structure, since the deal dies with the conflict. Campaign-donor conflicts in the first year attack the spoils economy that follows every election.

## Notes for adopting states

- This Part is the zero-corruption architecture; it may be adopted alone as an organic statute where full constitutional reform is not yet possible.
- Build the registers before the courts: disclosure, beneficial ownership and open contracting are the foundation the prosecutions stand on.
- Expect resistance precisely where the old system was most profitable - that resistance is the diagnostic.
