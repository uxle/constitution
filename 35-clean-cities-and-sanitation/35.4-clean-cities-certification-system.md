# Section 35.4 — Clean Cities Certification System

## Articles

**Article 35.4.1 (Certification Tiers).** A World Clean Cities Certification (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum) is awarded annually to municipalities meeting the cleanliness, recycling, and air/water-quality standards of Parts 13 and 35, published as a public league table.

**Article 35.4.2 (Funding Incentive).** Certified municipalities receive priority access to infrastructure grants under Part 08/23, creating a fiscal incentive for continued performance rather than relying on enforcement alone.

*Design Note: Public league tables have repeatedly proven more effective at sustaining civic pride and continued investment than penalties alone; this Article turns that observation into standing law.*

**Cross-references:** 35.1, 35.2, 08-public-finance, 23-infrastructure


## Implementation Detail

**Article 35.4.3 (Tier Thresholds).** Bronze requires meeting the universal floor of Article 35.1; Silver requires a 50% recycling rate; Gold requires 65% plus verified air- and water-quality compliance under Part 13; Platinum requires all Gold criteria plus a demonstrated 5-year improving trend.
