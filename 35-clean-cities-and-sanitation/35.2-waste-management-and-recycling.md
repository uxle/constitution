# Section 35.2 — Waste Management and Recycling

## Articles

**Article 35.2.1 (Universal Collection).** Every settlement, urban or highland (see Part 33), is entitled to regular waste collection as baseline infrastructure.

**Article 35.2.2 (Sorting and Recycling Mandate).** Municipalities must provide sorted collection (organic, recyclable, hazardous, general) and meet minimum recycling-rate targets set and reviewed by the Environmental Authority.

**Article 35.2.3 (Producer Responsibility).** Manufacturers bear extended responsibility for the end-of-life disposal of packaging and durable goods, funding collection and recycling systems rather than leaving the cost to municipalities alone.

*Design Note: Article 35.2.3 shifts a meaningful share of the cost of cleanliness away from municipal budgets and onto the producers whose packaging creates the waste, which is what makes the ambitious standard in 35.1 fiscally realistic.*

**Cross-references:** 35.1, 13-environment, 09-economy


## Implementation Detail

**Article 35.2.4 (Recycling Rate Targets).** Municipalities must reach a minimum 40% recycling rate within 5 years and 65% within 12 years of adoption, with the Environmental Authority publishing a public compliance league table.
