# Section 35.3 — Urban Cleanliness Culture and Education

## Articles

**Article 35.3.1 (Curriculum Requirement).** Civic cleanliness and environmental stewardship are taught as part of the compulsory civic-education curriculum under Part 11, from early childhood, on the premise that a clean society is a taught culture, not only an enforced rule.

**Article 35.3.2 (Community Participation Programs).** Municipalities are encouraged, and may be required by regional law, to run community clean-up and shared-space stewardship programs, particularly involving schools.

*Design Note: The most cited feature of famously clean cities is not the fine schedule but decades of civic education normalising the behavior — this section constitutionalizes that education, not just the enforcement.*

**Cross-references:** 35.1, 11-education/compulsory-education.md


## Implementation Detail

**Article 35.3.3 (Curriculum Hours).** Civic cleanliness and stewardship education must comprise at minimum 20 instructional hours per school year at primary level and 10 hours at secondary level.
