# Section 7.5 — Local Finance

> **Part 07 — Local Government** · Articles 7.5.1 – 7.5.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Section organizes local finance: own revenues, shared taxes and equalization transfers, the prohibition of unfunded mandates, transparent local budgets, and borrowing limits.*

## Article 7.5.1 — Own Revenues

1. Provinces and Municipalities shall have revenues of their own adequate to discharge their functions.
2. The law may authorize provincial and municipal taxes, fees, and charges; their bases and ceilings shall be fixed by law, and overlapping levies on the same base shall be coordinated.
3. Revenue-raising powers shall be exercised without discrimination against residents of other Provinces or Municipalities, consistently with Article 8.1.1 (Legality of Taxation).

## Article 7.5.2 — Shared Taxes and Equalization

1. The law may assign to Provinces and Municipalities defined shares in national taxes.
2. An equalization system shall reduce disparities in fiscal capacity among Provinces and Municipalities; its formula shall be objective, transparent, and fixed by law.
3. Transfers shall not be conditional on the political orientation of the recipient authority.

## Article 7.5.3 — No Unfunded Mandates

1. A law that imposes new obligations on Provinces or Municipalities shall provide the funding required to perform them.
2. Where the Parliament later reduces that funding, it shall correspondingly reduce the obligation.
3. A dispute over the adequacy of funding may be brought before the Constitutional Court under Article 5.3.3 (Exclusive Jurisdiction).

## Article 7.5.4 — Local Budgets and Transparency

1. Each Province and Municipality shall adopt an annual budget before its fiscal year begins.
2. Budgets and final accounts shall be published in a form that allows citizens to understand the sources and uses of funds.
3. Execution data shall be published at least quarterly.

## Article 7.5.5 — Local Borrowing

1. A Province or Municipality may borrow only within limits and procedures fixed by law, which shall distinguish investment borrowing from current financing.
2. Long-term borrowing shall serve investment purposes, and repayment obligations shall not exceed the ceilings fixed by law.
3. Borrowing by the Republic itself is governed by Part 08 (Public Finance).

## Design Note

*Adequate own revenues anchor local accountability: where services are paid from local sources, voters can price them politically. Equalization then corrects the geography of fiscal capacity so that equal citizenship does not depend on the wealth of a postal code, and the unfunded-mandate rule closes the oldest loophole in devolution, the center legislating costs downward. Borrowing limits protect both local solvency and the credit of the Republic.*
