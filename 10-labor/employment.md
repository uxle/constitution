# Section 10.1 — Employment

> **Part 10 — Labor** · Articles 10.1.1 – 10.1.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section governs the individual employment relationship — free choice of work, the prohibition of forced labor, security against arbitrary dismissal, and the extension of protection to every form of work.*

## Article 10.1.1 — Free Choice of Work

1. Every person has the right to work and to a free choice of employment, and no person shall be barred from lawful work except as provided by law for the protection of others.
2. Access to employment, vocational training, and advancement shall be open to all on the basis of qualification, and no person shall be discriminated against in any aspect of employment on a ground prohibited by Article 1.2.2 (Prohibition of discrimination).
3. The State shall pursue full, productive, and freely chosen employment through measures that sustain economic activity and develop skills.

## Article 10.1.2 — Prohibition of Forced Labor

1. Forced or compulsory labor is prohibited in every form, in fulfillment of Article 1.9.1 (Prohibition of forced labor), and no person shall be required to work under threat of penalty or without offering freely.
2. The State shall take effective measures to prevent, detect, and punish forced labor, trafficking for labor exploitation, and debt bondage, including within supply chains.

## Article 10.1.3 — Security Against Dismissal

1. No employee shall be dismissed except for a reason recognized as valid by law, established by a fair procedure, and subject to review by an impartial body, consistent with Article 1.9.4 (Equality and security in employment).
2. Termination shall carry notice or compensation as prescribed by law; collective dismissals shall require prior consultation with worker representatives and notification of the competent authority.
3. Employment shall not be terminated, downgraded, or penalized because the employee exercised a constitutional or statutory right, reported a violation, or refused an unlawful instruction.

## Article 10.1.4 — Atypical and Platform Workers

1. Protection under this Part shall not depend on the form of the contract; every person performing work for another under an employment relationship, or in circumstances resembling one, shall enjoy the minimum standards of this Part.
2. The law shall presume an employment relationship where control, economic dependence, or subordination is established, and shall prevent misclassification.
3. Platform, domestic, seasonal, and home-based work shall be expressly covered by implementing legislation.

## Article 10.1.5 — Public Employment Services

1. The State shall maintain a public employment service, free of charge to workers, providing placement, labor-market information, vocational guidance, and reintegration support.
2. Private placement agencies may operate only under license and shall not charge fees to workers.

## Design Note

*Dismissal rules follow ILO Convention No. 158, whose sparse ratification shows that job security remains contested ground. Presuming employment from control and dependence shifts the burden to the party best placed to disclose the true arrangement. The trade-off is between dismissal protection and flexibility, resolved by requiring valid reasons while leaving notice and compensation levels to Parliament.*
