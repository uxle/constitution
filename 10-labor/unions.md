# Section 10.6 — Unions

> **Part 10 — Labor** · Articles 10.6.1 – 10.6.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section protects freedom of association at work: the formation and autonomy of unions, freedom from employer domination, and protection from anti-union discrimination.*

## Article 10.6.1 — Formation of Unions

1. Workers and employers, without distinction whatsoever, have the right to establish and, subject only to the rules of the organization concerned, to join organizations of their own choosing without previous authorization, in fulfillment of Article 1.9.3 (Trade unions and collective bargaining).
2. No person shall be required to join or to refrain from joining an organization of workers or employers, and any arrangement compelling either is void.

## Article 10.6.2 — Union Autonomy

1. Workers' and employers' organizations shall draw up their constitutions and rules, elect their representatives freely by secret ballot, organize their administration, and formulate their programs without interference by public authorities or by each other.
2. An organization may be dissolved or suspended only in accordance with law and by final judgment of a court, and its assets shall pass to a like organization.

## Article 10.6.3 — Employer Non-Interference

1. Employers shall not dominate or finance unions, shall not interfere in their administration, and shall not discriminate among workers by reason of the organization to which they belong.
2. Yellow-dog arrangements, under which a worker renounces union membership as a condition of employment, are void.

## Article 10.6.4 — Protection from Anti-Union Discrimination

1. No worker shall be refused employment, dismissed, or subjected to any detriment by reason of union membership or lawful union activity, consistent with Article 1.9.4 (Equality and security in employment).
2. Remedies shall include reinstatement and compensation sufficient to deter violation.

## Article 10.6.5 — Federations and Pluralism

1. Organizations shall have the right to federate, confederate, and affiliate with international organizations of workers and employers.
2. The pluralism of organizations is guaranteed, and the law shall not reserve representation to a single confederation.

## Design Note

*The section follows ILO Conventions No. 87 and No. 98, the standards that define freedom of association at work for supervisory bodies worldwide. Requiring judicial dissolution and successorship of assets prevents the historical device of dissolving troublesome unions under administrative discretion. The ban on employer domination and the voiding of yellow-dog clauses answer the two oldest instruments of sham unionism. The accepted trade-off is unqualified pluralism, which preserves worker choice but can fragment bargaining strength across competing organizations.*
