# Section 10.8 — Unemployment Protection

> **Part 10 — Labor** · Articles 10.8.1 – 10.8.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This section protects workers when work ends: insurance against job loss, activation services and training rights, and a floor of assistance for those the labor market leaves out.*

## Article 10.8.1 — Right to Unemployment Protection

1. Every worker who loses employment through no fault of that worker has the right to unemployment protection sufficient to maintain an adequate standard of living while seeking new work with dignity.
2. Protection under this Article forms part of the social security system of the Republic and shall be coordinated with Article 22.1.1 (Right to Social Security).

## Article 10.8.2 — Unemployment Insurance

1. Parliament shall maintain a compulsory unemployment insurance scheme, funded by contributions of employers and workers and, where necessary, by the State, providing benefits proportionate to prior earnings and limited in duration by law.
2. Benefits shall be payable promptly, shall be adjusted to preserve their value, and shall not be reduced as a penalty except for defined misconduct established by fair procedure.
3. Contributions shall be segregated from general revenue, and accrued entitlements shall be portable across sectors and Provinces as prescribed by law.

## Article 10.8.3 — Activation Services

1. Every beneficiary has the right to placement services, vocational guidance, and support for relocation or self-employment, and the corresponding duty to remain available for and to seek suitable work.
2. Work is suitable only if it corresponds to the capacity, prior occupation, and reasonable prospects of the beneficiary and offers conditions not less favorable than those prevailing locally.

## Article 10.8.4 — Training Rights

1. Every beneficiary has the right to vocational training and retraining under Article 11.5.5 (Retraining for Displaced Workers), with income support where the training requires it.
2. The State shall maintain active labor-market programs, evaluated for effectiveness, with priority for regions and groups facing structural displacement.

## Article 10.8.5 — Assistance of Last Resort

1. A person whose entitlement is exhausted and who remains without work shall have access to assistance of last resort, without work requirements that would compel acceptance of degrading conditions.
2. The State shall monitor long-term unemployment and act to prevent its intergenerational transmission.

## Design Note

*The scheme tracks ILO Conventions No. 102 and No. 168 on minimum social security and protection against unemployment, while the balance of rights and duties reflects the flexicurity model associated with the Nordic systems: generous benefits, strong activation, and manageable dismissal barriers. Segregating contributions from general revenue protects the fund from raiding during fiscal stress. The suitability test limits what the State may demand before sanctions. The accepted trade-off is conditionality — work-search duties respect contributors but risk coercing the desperate, so the last-resort clause caps what may be demanded.*

**Cross-references:** Article 22.1.1 (Right to Social Security); Article 11.5.5 (Retraining for Displaced Workers); Article 10.1.5 (Public Employment Services).
