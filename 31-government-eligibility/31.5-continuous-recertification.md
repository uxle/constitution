# Section 31.5 — Continuous Re-Certification

## Articles

**Article 31.5.1 (Term-Linked Review).** Every officeholder undergoes the education refresher (31.2.4), a repeat psychological screening (31.3), and a repeat fitness review (31.4) at least once per elected term, and more frequently for offices with emergency powers.

**Article 31.5.2 (No Automatic Removal).** A single flagged review triggers a structured improvement or leave process, not automatic removal; only a sustained, appeal-exhausted failure triggers Article 31.7 disqualification.

**Article 31.5.3 (Transparency of Process, Not Content).** The public is told that a review occurred and its pass/fail outcome; clinical and personal detail remains protected under 31.3.4.

*Design Note: Power tends to change people, sometimes for the worse; a single entry exam is not enough. Re-certification treats fitness to govern as a standard to be maintained, not a badge earned once and kept forever.*

**Cross-references:** 31.2, 31.3, 31.4, 31.7


## Implementation Detail

**Article 31.5.4 (Improvement Process Length).** A structured improvement or leave process under Article 31.5.2 runs for a fixed period of 6 months, after which a second review determines certification or referral to Article 31.7 disqualification proceedings.
