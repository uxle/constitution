# Section 31.1 — General Principle of Eligibility

No person may hold governing office — legislative, executive, or judicial, at any level from a village council to the World Assembly — on the strength of birth, wealth, popularity, or force alone. Every officeholder must clear the same three gates: **education**, **psychological fitness**, and **physical fitness**, defined in 31.2–31.4 and re-applied throughout a career under 31.5.

## Articles

**Article 31.1.1 (Scope).** This Part applies to every elected, appointed, or hereditary person who exercises binding governmental power, including ministers, judges, legislators, regional governors, and — for any governing role beyond ceremonial duties — members of the Royal Family.

**Article 31.1.2 (No Exemptions by Birth).** Being born into the Royal Family, a political dynasty, or a wealthy family confers no exemption from Articles 31.2–31.4. A monarch's child is a candidate like any other before they may hold a governing office; see 31.6.

**Article 31.1.3 (No Exemption by Election).** Winning a popular election qualifies a candidate to stand, but does not itself satisfy the eligibility gates. A candidate must clear 31.2–31.4 *before* their name may appear on a ballot or appointment list.

**Article 31.1.4 (Independent Administration).** The gates are administered by the Integrity Branch's Governance Eligibility Board (see Part 37), never by the government the candidate hopes to join, to prevent incumbents from writing their own exam.

*Design Note: The core idea this Part protects is simple — power should never be easier to get than a driving license. The three-gate model borrows the "competence before authority" logic used for pilots, surgeons, and judges in most existing legal systems, and extends it, for the first time, to political power itself.*

## Implementation Detail

**Article 31.1.5 (Timeline).** Candidacy applications open no later than 180 days before an election; the eligibility gates (31.2–31.4) must be completed and certified no later than 60 days before the vote, giving candidates a fixed 120-day certification window.

**Article 31.1.6 (Cost Coverage).** All three gates are administered free of charge to the candidate, funded by the Integrity Branch's budget, so that cost can never be a barrier to standing for office.
