# Section 30.5 — Protected Provisions

> **Part 30 — Schedules** · Articles 30.5.1 – 30.5.5 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Schedule lists the entrenched provisions that, in addition to the ordinary amendment majorities, require a national referendum majority under Part 25 (Constitutional Process).*

## Article 30.5.1 — Entrenchment

1. An amendment of a provision listed in Article 30.5.2 requires, in addition to a two-thirds majority of the members of each House, approval by a majority of the valid votes cast in a national referendum.
2. The referendum is conducted under Article 6.7.3 (Mandatory Referendum on Entrenched Amendments).
3. A purported amendment adopted without the required referendum is void.

## Article 30.5.2 — Protected Content

1. The entrenched provisions are:
   (a) the republican and democratic form of the State, including the parliamentary order and the principle of free, periodic elections under Article 0.6.2 (Free and Periodic Elections);
   (b) human dignity under Article 0.7.1 (Inviolability of Human Dignity);
   (c) equality and the prohibition of discrimination under Section 1.2;
   (d) judicial independence under Section 5.1;
   (e) the non-derogable rights under Article 1.1.5 (Non-Derogable Rights);
   (f) the amendment rules of Part 25 (Constitutional Process);
   (g) the amendment freeze of Article 26.5.5 (Amendment Freeze);
   (h) this Schedule and the entrenchment rule of Article 30.5.1.
2. The protection of clause 1 extends to repeal and to any alteration diminishing a protected provision's substance.

## Article 30.5.3 — Scope of the Protection

1. An amendment touching protected content in any respect falls under this Schedule, irrespective of its direction or of the proportion affected.
2. An amendment that only extends protection, without altering existing substance, follows the ordinary tier.
3. The addition of further provisions to this Schedule follows the entrenched tier.

## Article 30.5.4 — Referendum Requirements

1. The question submitted to the Electorate shall reproduce the substance of the amendment faithfully and comprehensibly.
2. The referendum is administered by the Electoral Commission, under Article 3.5.3 (Exclusive Administration).
3. The amendment enters into force only after publication of the certified referendum result.

## Article 30.5.5 — Verification

1. The Constitutional Court shall verify, before proclamation, that the requirements of this Schedule and of Part 25 (Constitutional Process) have been observed.
2. The Court shall decide a verification proceeding with priority.

## Design Note

*Entrenchment protects not against change but against unreflective change: the referendum tier ensures that the provisions constituting the Republic, its form, dignity, equality, courts, and the hard core of rights, can be altered only when the whole electorate, not merely a supermajority of its representatives, bears the responsibility. The content list parallels the eternity-clause tradition of the German Basic Law while keeping every protected item concrete rather than open-ended, which is why equality is protected through Section 1.2 and judicial independence through Section 5.1. Including the amendment rules of Part 25 and the emergency amendment freeze prevents the tiers themselves from being lowered, and the verification role of the Constitutional Court gives the tier an enforcer, making the referendum requirement a legal fact rather than a convention.*
