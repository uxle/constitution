# Section 30.1 — Oath of Office

> **Part 30 — Schedules** · Articles 30.1.1 – 30.1.6 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Schedule prescribes the oaths and solemn affirmations of the principal offices; the affirmation has the same effect as the oath.*

## Article 30.1.1 — Form and Effect

1. The oaths of this Schedule are taken publicly upon taking office before the organ designated by law, the oath of the President before the Constitutional Court.
2. Every oath may be taken as a solemn affirmation with the same effect, omitting "swear" and "So help me God".
3. A person who declines the oath or affirmation does not enter upon office until taking it.
4. The oath for further offices is fixed by law in the form of this Schedule.

## Article 30.1.2 — Oath of the President

The President takes this oath:

"I, (name), do swear that I will faithfully discharge the office of President of the Republic; uphold and defend the Constitution and the laws; respect, protect, and fulfill the rights of every person; and devote my powers to the well-being and democracy of the Republic. So help me God."

## Article 30.1.3 — Oath of the Prime Minister and Ministers

The Prime Minister and ministers take this oath:

"I, (name), do swear that I will faithfully discharge the duties of my office; uphold and defend the Constitution and the laws; act in the interest of the Republic and all its people, without fear or favour, affection or ill-will; and preserve the confidentiality my office requires. So help me God."

## Article 30.1.4 — Oath of Judges

Every judge takes this oath:

"I, (name), do swear that I will administer justice impartially and conscientiously, according to this Constitution and the laws; decide without fear, favour, affection, or ill-will; and preserve the independence and dignity of my office. So help me God."

## Article 30.1.5 — Oath of Members of Parliament

A member of the Parliament takes this oath before taking the seat:

"I, (name), do swear that I will bear true allegiance to the Constitution and the people of the Republic; faithfully discharge the duties of a member; and conduct myself in the public interest. So help me God."

## Article 30.1.6 — Oath of Members of the Armed Forces

Members of the armed forces take this oath upon entry into service, under Article 18.2.2 (Oath to the Constitution):

"I, (name), do swear that I will respect and defend the Constitution, the laws, and the democratic order of the Republic; obey only lawful orders; and faithfully serve the Republic at all times. So help me God."

## Design Note

*Oaths bind the office holder to the Constitution and the people rather than to a person or party, which is why the service-member's text forbids obeying unlawful orders, mirroring Section 18.2. The affirmation alternative keeps public office open to those whose conscience refuses an oath. The presidential oath is taken before the Constitutional Court, the institution that guards the order being sworn, and judges swear to preserve the independence of their office itself.*
