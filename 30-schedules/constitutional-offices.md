# Section 30.3 — Constitutional Offices

> **Part 30 — Schedules** · Articles 30.3.1 – 30.3.4 · Adopted — v1.0.0

*This Schedule summarizes in one table the constitutional offices with appointment, term, and removal; governing articles prevail over the summary.*

## Article 30.3.1 — Scope and Effect

1. The table in Article 30.3.2 lists the Republic's constitutional offices.
2. The table is declaratory; a divergent governing article prevails.
3. A holder of a listed office is a senior public official for Section 27.3 (Asset Disclosures).

## Article 30.3.2 — Table of Constitutional Offices

1. The constitutional offices are the following:

| Office | Appointment | Term | Removal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| President | Joint sitting, both Houses | Six years, single | Article 4.1.6 (Impeachment) |
| Prime Minister | Election by the Lower House | While confidence holds | Article 4.2.4 (Constructive Vote of No Confidence) |
| Ministers | President, on the Prime Minister's proposal | Cabinet mandate | Dismissal on that proposal, Article 4.3.1 (Appointment of Ministers) |
| Judges, Constitutional Court | President, on binding recommendation | Twelve years, single | Article 5.1.5 (Removal for Cause) |
| Judges, other courts | President, on binding recommendation | To statutory retirement age | Article 5.1.5 (Removal for Cause) |
| Public Prosecutor | As provided by law | Seven years, single | For cause, by law |
| Auditor-General | Parliament, by supermajority | Single term | For cause, by the same supermajority |
| Heads of Part 20 institutions | Open process, fixed terms | Fixed, staggered where collegiate | Cause only, qualified majority |

2. Judicial appointments rest on the Judicial Service Commission's binding recommendation under Article 5.5.4 (Binding Recommendations).
3. Staggered terms of collegiate institutions follow Article 20.4.1 (Composition and Staggered Terms).

## Article 30.3.3 — Vacancies and Acting Capacity

1. Succession to, and vacancy in, a listed office follow the governing article or, where silent, the law.
2. An acting holder takes the oath or affirmation under Section 30.1 before acting and bears the office's duties while so acting.
3. Acting capacity shall not exceed the period fixed by law or, in default, ninety days.

## Article 30.3.4 — Amendment of This Schedule

1. This Schedule is amended by simple majority under the schedule tier of Part 25 (Constitutional Process).
2. An amendment altering the substance of a constitutional office follows the tier applicable to the establishing provision.
3. A certified consolidated text of this Schedule is published after each amendment.

## Design Note

*Gathering the offices into one table serves reader, voter, and drafter: appointment, term, and removal are the three levers of every independence arrangement, and seeing them side by side exposes any lever left loose. The table is expressly declaratory because duplicating rules invites divergence. Classifying the holders as senior public officials links the schedule to Part 27 integrity regimes without re-listing anyone, and the two-tier amendment rule keeps a schedule edit from moving an office into or out of constitutional protection.*

**Cross-references:** Article 5.5.4 (Binding Recommendations); Article 20.4.1 (Composition and Staggered Terms); Article 4.1.6 (Impeachment).
